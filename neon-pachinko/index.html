Game and local data
You can play without a game account, identity verification or advertising. Progress, collections, game settings and daily reward records are stored on your device. Language and advertising choices are stored separately from gameplay. This version has no cloud saves, in-app purchases, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Beads, baskets and cards have no cash value and cannot be bought for money, traded or redeemed for real-world prizes. Android app backup is disabled in this build.

Optional rewarded advertising
Only after you choose to enable ads does the game initialize Unity LevelPlay and preload rewarded ads. LevelPlay includes ironSource Ads and integrated Ad Quality monitoring; the Unity Ads SDK and adapter are also included. Actual ad sources depend on the networks enabled for this app and their availability. Ad Quality initializes with LevelPlay. This may cause network requests before you press a watch-ad button. Ads are displayed only when you request them. Continuing without ads keeps ordinary gameplay available.

Advertising providers may process IP-derived approximate location, installation/user and advertising identifiers (including Google advertising ID where available), device or other IDs, device/app/system and network information, ad and app interactions, other actions, and diagnostic information. These support advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, app functionality, ad-quality monitoring, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Providers may share data with their service providers and advertising partners and process it outside your country under their policies. We do not upload your local game save or collection through this integration. The SDK providers disclose encrypted data transmission.

Enabling ads is not consent to personalized advertising. This build requests GDPR consent=false, CCPA do-not-sell=true and child-directed treatment=false. These flags do not mean the SDKs process no information. The game does not request contacts, microphone, camera, precise device location, real-name verification or identity documents.

Rewards and your controls
The optional shop video awards one basket (500 beads) only after a genuine SDK reward confirmation and valid game conditions. The game limits this to ten times per game day. Closing without earning a reward, unavailable ads and loading failures do not earn rewards.

You can disable advertising in Settings and continue playing. This stops new game-initiated ad requests and cancels pending requests; it cannot erase data already sent or guarantee every internal operation of an already initialized SDK immediately stops. A currently displayed full-screen ad may still need to be closed. Close and restart the app after disabling ads to end that SDK process. Future launches keep ads disabled unless you enable them again. If the choice cannot be saved, the game displays an error; retry before restarting.

Time checks and policy hosting
After entering gameplay, the app may send an HTTPS HEAD request to this app's policy page to obtain the server's Date header for daily rewards, even if ads are disabled. GitHub Pages receives connection information and logs visitor IP addresses for security. No save data is sent. The game uses device time if the request fails; failure does not prevent play. The game-day boundary remains 04:00 UTC+8. Opening public policy/provider links also sends ordinary connection information to those sites. These pages contain no developer-added analytics or advertising.

Deletion, retention and privacy requests
Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove local game progress and preferences. This is irreversible and there is no cloud copy or game account to restore. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID through Android's supported settings is separate from deleting local game data. Deleting local data does not delete information already held by a provider.

Local data remains until it is deleted or replaced. Provider-held information follows the retention practices and rights processes in the providers' notices. For access, correction, deletion, objection or other privacy questions, contact qijiangyuan@gmail.com; you may also use the providers' privacy request channels below. If you contact us, we process your message and the contact information you choose to supply to respond to the request. Do not send passwords, government ID or unnecessary sensitive information. Available rights depend on your jurisdiction.

Audience, security and changes
This game is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. It has no chat or account-profile upload feature. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. We update this policy when the integration or data practices change materially and request a new advertising choice when required.

游戏与本地数据
无需游戏账号、身份验证或广告即可游玩。游戏进度、收藏、设置与每日奖励记录保存在设备上。语言和广告选择与游戏进度分开保存。本版本没有云存档、应用内购买、Firebase Analytics 或 Firebase Crashlytics。珠子、篮子和积分卡没有现金价值，不能付费购买、交易或兑换现实奖品。本构建已关闭 Android 应用备份。

可选激励广告
只有选择开启广告后，游戏才初始化 Unity LevelPlay 并预加载激励广告。LevelPlay 包含 ironSource Ads 与集成的 Ad Quality 广告质量监测；本包也包含 Unity Ads SDK 及适配器。实际广告来源取决于本应用后台启用的网络及可用性。Ad Quality 随 LevelPlay 初始化，因此可能在点击观看广告按钮前发起网络请求。广告仅在主动请求时展示。不启用广告仍可正常游玩。

广告供应商可能处理由 IP 推断的大致位置、安装/用户与广告标识符（包括可用时的 Google 广告 ID）、设备或其他 ID、设备/应用/系统和网络信息、广告与应用互动、其他操作和诊断信息。这些数据用于广告展示与衡量、分析、应用功能、广告质量监测、反欺诈、安全和合规。供应商可按其政策与服务提供商及广告合作伙伴共享数据，并在您所在国家之外处理数据。本接入不会上传本地存档或收藏。SDK 供应商披露其数据传输经过加密。

开启广告不代表同意个性化广告。本构建传入 GDPR consent=false、CCPA do-not-sell=true、child-directed=false；这些标志不代表 SDK 不处理任何信息。游戏不请求通讯录、麦克风、相机、精确设备位置、实名验证或身份证件。

奖励和您的控制
商店的可选广告只有收到真实 SDK 奖励确认、且满足游戏条件时才提供一篮（500 颗珠子），每游戏日最多十次。未获奖励便关闭、广告不可用或加载失败均不发奖。

您可在设置中关闭广告并继续游玩。这会停止游戏发起的新广告请求并取消待处理请求，但不能删除已经发送的数据，也无法保证已经初始化的 SDK 所有内部操作立即停止。正在展示的全屏广告可能仍需关闭。关闭广告后退出并重启应用可结束该 SDK 进程。后续启动保持关闭，除非再次主动开启。如果选择保存失败，游戏会提示错误；请在重启前重试。

网络对时与政策托管
进入游戏后，即使关闭了广告，应用仍可能向本应用的政策页面发送 HTTPS HEAD 请求，读取服务器 Date 响应头以计算每日奖励。GitHub Pages 会接收连接信息，并为安全目的记录访客 IP 地址。不发送游戏存档。请求失败时使用设备时间，不妨碍游戏。游戏日边界保持为 UTC+8 04:00。打开公开政策或供应商链接也会向相应网站发送常规连接信息。这些页面没有开发者添加的分析或广告。

删除、保留与隐私请求
在 Android 设置中清除应用存储，或卸载应用，可删除本地进度与偏好。此操作不可逆，本游戏没有可用于恢复的云副本或游戏账号。通过 Android 支持的设置重置或删除广告 ID，与删除本地游戏数据是不同操作。删除本地数据不会删除供应商已经持有的信息。

本地数据会保留到被删除或替换。供应商持有的信息遵循其政策中的保留期限和权利流程。如需访问、更正、删除、反对处理，或咨询隐私问题，请联系 qijiangyuan@gmail.com；也可使用下列供应商隐私请求渠道。联系我们时，我们会处理您主动提供的消息与联系信息以回复请求。请勿发送密码、身份证件或不必要的敏感信息。可行使的权利取决于您的司法辖区。

受众、安全与更新
本游戏面向18岁及以上成人，并非儿童定向。没有聊天或账号资料上传功能。任何存储或传输方式都不能保证绝对安全。接入或数据行为发生重大变化时会更新本政策，并在需要时重新征求广告选择。
