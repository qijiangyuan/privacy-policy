Neon Pachinko: Arcade — Data Deletion

Privacy policy · Data deletion · 简体中文

Riverstar Games · com.jcgame.danzhuji.global · Updated 9 November 2026

Delete local game data

The game has no game account or cloud save. In Android Settings, select Apps → Neon Pachinko: Arcade → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This deletes local progress, collections, settings, language and advertising preferences. Deletion is irreversible; there is no cloud copy to restore.

Disable advertising

In the game's Settings, turn off advertising and restart the app. You can continue playing. This stops future game-initiated advertising requests; it does not delete information previously received by providers. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings is a separate action.

Request deletion of provider-held information

For help or a privacy request, contact qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the game name and a description of your request. Do not send passwords or identity documents. We will explain the information reasonably needed to identify your request; there is no game account identifier. Data already held by advertising/hosting providers is subject to their retention and rights processes.

简体中文

本游戏无需游戏账号，也没有云存档。在 Android 设置中选择“应用 → Neon Pachinko: Arcade → 存储 → 清除数据”，或卸载应用，可删除本地进度、收藏、设置、语言与广告选择。删除后无法从云端恢复。

在游戏设置中关闭广告并重启，可停止后续游戏广告请求并继续游玩。此操作和清除本地数据都不会删除第三方此前已接收的信息。重置或删除 Android 广告 ID 是独立操作。

如需协助或申请处理个人数据，请联系 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，说明游戏名称及请求内容；请勿发送密码、身份证件或不必要的敏感资料。我们会说明核对请求所需的合理信息。第三方数据的保留和权利申请流程见上方官方政策链接。
