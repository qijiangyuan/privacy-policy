English

This policy explains how jcgame handles information for Relic Delvers: Roguelike RPG, also called Relic Delvers and 遗迹远征队 (the “Game”), Android package com.jcgame.relicdelvers.global, and when you contact us.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores expedition progress, characters, equipment, skills, talents, permanent upgrades, unlocks, reward-claim records and local challenge records on your device. Music and sound settings, language preference and startup privacy acceptance are also stored locally. English is the initial language; Simplified Chinese can be selected in settings. The Game does not require account registration, provide a developer-operated cloud-save service, player chat or an online leaderboard, or offer real-money in-app purchases. Its game shop uses in-game resources.

The Game does not upload your complete saves to a jcgame gameplay server. This Android build disables the app's Android backup setting. We do not provide a cloud copy of your progress; removing app data can permanently remove progress.

2. Advertising services

Optional rewarded videos use Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, with the integrated Ad Quality service. After you accept the first-launch privacy notice, the Game initializes advertising and preloads ads. A choice not to watch a rewarded video does not stop all advertising-service processing once those services are initialized.

These services may process advertising, app-set, installation and other pseudonymous identifiers; device, operating-system, app and network information; IP addresses and approximate location derived from them; ad views, interactions and reward events; advertising performance and impression revenue; ad-content metadata and diagnostics. Processing supports ad delivery and measurement, rewarded-ad operation, ad-quality monitoring, troubleshooting, fraud prevention and security. Providers may share information with their service providers and advertising partners under their policies. Advertising dashboards may provide us with performance and quality reports.

The Game does not provide your name, email address or a custom game account ID to the ad SDKs. This does not mean the SDKs have no identifiers. The Game does not request precise-location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Advertising providers' own data handling remains governed by their policies and applicable privacy settings.

Provider information and request channels: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Unity player privacy FAQ, and LevelPlay data disclosure.

3. Privacy choices

Accepting the startup notice does not authorize personalized advertising. The Game currently sends a negative GDPR advertising-consent signal and a do-not-sell signal to LevelPlay. These signals do not mean that contextual advertising, diagnostics and fraud-prevention processing stop. Declining the startup notice exits the app. Changing language or opening this policy does not accept the notice.

You can use available Android advertising-ID and ad-privacy controls, and privacy controls offered within an ad where available. Clearing the Game's app data resets its saved startup acceptance and also deletes local progress. These device actions do not automatically erase records already held by advertising providers. For provider-controlled information, use the provider's request channels or contact us for help identifying the appropriate provider.

4. Firebase and future changes

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled for the first overseas release. The Game does not enable their analytics or crash-report collection in this release, even though SDK libraries remain packaged. If a later version enables them or adds another service, we will update the policy and relevant app disclosures to reflect that version.

5. Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we receive the sender address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, provide support and handle privacy requests. We retain support information only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Local game data remains until you clear it, uninstall the app, or the Game updates or replaces the relevant local data through normal gameplay. Advertising providers apply their own retention schedules, including any security or legal requirements.

There is no Game account to delete. To remove local data, use Android Settings → Apps → Relic Delvers → Storage → Clear storage/data (labels vary). To request deletion of support information or relevant provider-held information, see our Data Deletion instructions. Clearing or uninstalling the Game is separate from asking a provider to delete previously transmitted information.

6. Security, international processing and privacy rights

We use app-local storage and the services described above. Providers describe their transport-security measures in their public disclosures. Service providers may process information in countries other than where you live, subject to applicable safeguards and their policies.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to your local authority. Contact us to make a request concerning information we handle. We may ask for limited verification details needed to locate and address the request; do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We respond as required by applicable law. For information a provider controls independently, use its privacy channels.

7. Children and contact

The Game is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that requires attention, contact us and identify the Game and the relevant request.

Publisher and privacy contact: jcgame, qijiangyuan@gmail.com. Include Relic Delvers Privacy in the subject. We will revise this page and its update date when our practices change.

简体中文

本政策说明 jcgame 在《遗迹远征队》／Relic Delvers: Roguelike RPG（也称 Relic Delvers，以下简称“游戏”），Android 包名 com.jcgame.relicdelvers.global，以及您联系我们时如何处理信息。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日。

1. 保存在本机的信息

游戏把远征进度、角色、装备、技能、天赋、永久强化、解锁、奖励领取记录及本地挑战记录保存在设备上。音乐和音效设置、语言偏好及首次隐私接受记录也保存在本机。首次语言为英文，可在设置中选择简体中文。游戏不要求注册账号，不提供开发者运营的云存档、玩家聊天或在线排行榜，也没有真实货币应用内购买；游戏内商店使用虚拟资源。

游戏不会把完整存档上传到 jcgame 的玩法服务器。此 Android 构建关闭应用的 Android 备份设置，我们不提供进度的云端副本；删除应用数据可能永久丢失进度。

2. 广告服务

可选激励视频使用 Unity LevelPlay、Unity Ads 及集成的 Ad Quality 服务。接受首次隐私提示后，游戏会初始化广告服务并预加载广告。初始化后，选择不观看激励视频不代表广告服务的所有数据处理都会停止。

这些服务可能处理广告、应用集、安装及其他假名化标识；设备、操作系统、应用和网络信息；IP 地址及其推导的大致位置；广告展示、互动和奖励事件；广告表现及展示收益；广告素材元数据与诊断信息。用途包括广告投放和衡量、激励广告运行、广告质量监测、故障排查、防止欺诈和安全。供应商可能按照其政策向服务提供商和广告合作伙伴共享信息，其广告后台也可能向我们提供表现和质量报告。

游戏不会向广告 SDK 提供您的姓名、邮箱或自定义游戏账号 ID，但 SDK 仍可能使用自己的标识。游戏不请求精确位置、通讯录、相机或麦克风权限。广告供应商自身的信息处理受其政策及适用的隐私设置约束。请参阅 Unity 游戏玩家与应用用户隐私政策、Unity 玩家隐私常见问题及 LevelPlay 数据披露。

3. 隐私选择

接受首次提示不代表授权个性化广告。游戏目前向 LevelPlay 传递 GDPR 广告同意为否和“不出售”信号；这些信号不代表情境广告、诊断及反欺诈处理停止。拒绝首次提示会退出应用。切换语言或打开政策不会接受提示。

您可使用 Android 中可用的广告标识和广告隐私控制，以及广告内提供的隐私选项。清除游戏的应用数据可重置首次接受记录，但也会删除本地进度。这些设备操作不会自动删除供应商已经持有的信息。对于供应商控制的数据，可使用其隐私请求渠道，或联系我们协助识别相应供应商。

4. Firebase 与未来变更

首个海外发行版本关闭 Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics。 尽管相关 SDK 库仍随包提供，此版本不启用其分析或崩溃报告收集。后续版本若启用这些功能或增加服务，我们会更新政策及相应应用披露。

5. 支持联系、保留与删除

您发送邮件时，我们会收到发件地址、邮件内容及您主动提供的附件，用于回复、支持和处理隐私请求。支持信息仅在实现这些目的及履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。本地游戏数据在您清除、卸载，或游戏通过正常玩法更新／替换相应数据前留在设备上。广告供应商按其自身保留安排处理数据，包括安全或法律要求。

游戏没有需要删除的玩家账号。删除本地数据可使用 Android“设置 → 应用 → Relic Delvers → 存储 → 清除存储空间／数据”（名称因设备而异）。有关支持信息及供应商数据的请求请查看 数据删除说明。清除或卸载游戏与请求供应商删除已传输数据是不同步骤。

6. 安全、跨境处理与隐私权利

我们使用应用本地存储及上述服务；供应商在公开披露中说明其传输安全措施。服务提供商可能在您所在地区以外处理信息，并适用相应保护措施及其政策。

根据适用法律，您可能享有访问、更正、删除、获取信息副本、反对或限制处理、在基于同意处理时撤回同意，以及向当地监管机构投诉等权利。有关我们处理的信息可直接联系我们。为定位和处理请求，我们可能要求必要且有限的验证信息；请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感资料。我们会按适用法律回复。供应商独立控制的信息请通过其隐私渠道提出请求。

7. 儿童与联系方式

本游戏非面向儿童。若您认为儿童提供了需要处理的个人信息，请联系我们并注明游戏及相关请求。
