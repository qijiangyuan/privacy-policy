English简体中文Privacy Policy

English

Relic Delvers has no player account to delete. Local game data and information previously held by advertising providers or in support emails require different actions.

1. Delete device-local information

In Android, open Settings → Apps → Relic Delvers → Storage → Clear storage/data. Device labels may differ. This removes local progress, upgrades, unlocks, claimed-reward records, settings, language choice and startup privacy acceptance. Uninstalling also removes this app's local data. The Game does not provide a developer-operated cloud copy and disables its Android app backup setting.

Local progress cannot be recovered from us after deletion. This action does not erase data already held by an advertising provider.

2. Request deletion of support or provider-held information

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with Relic Delvers Data Deletion in the subject. Identify the Game and explain the information or request involved. We may need limited verification details to locate and address it; do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We handle information under our control and can help identify the relevant service provider.

For information independently controlled by Unity advertising services, use the rights/request channels in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy or privacy controls available in an ad. Providers may retain information as required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Deletion of local data, resetting an advertising ID and uninstalling are separate from a provider deletion request.

3. Stop further use

Uninstall the Game to stop processing through this installed app. To reset its startup notice, clear app data, which also deletes progress. In this release, declining that notice exits the app; there is no setting that disables all advertising-service processing while continuing to play after initialization. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled.

简体中文

《遗迹远征队》没有需要删除的玩家账号。本地游戏数据、广告供应商已经持有的信息及支持邮件，需要分别处理。

1. 删除设备上的本地信息

打开 Android“设置 → 应用 → Relic Delvers → 存储 → 清除存储空间／数据”（名称因设备而异）。这会删除本地进度、强化、解锁、奖励领取记录、设置、语言选择和首次隐私接受记录。卸载也会删除应用本地数据。游戏没有开发者云端副本，并关闭此 Android 应用的备份设置。

删除后我们无法为您恢复本地进度。 这些操作不会删除广告供应商已持有的信息。

2. 请求删除支持信息或供应商数据

发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，主题注明 Relic Delvers Data Deletion／遗迹远征队数据删除，说明游戏及涉及的信息或请求。我们可能要求定位和处理请求所必需的有限验证信息；请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感资料。我们处理自身控制的信息，并可协助识别相应服务提供商。

Unity 广告服务独立控制的信息，请通过 Unity 游戏玩家与应用用户隐私政策 的权利请求渠道或广告内可用隐私选项处理。供应商可能按安全、反欺诈或法律要求保留信息。删除本地数据、重置广告标识、卸载和向供应商提出删除请求是不同操作。

3. 停止后续使用

卸载可停止通过这份已安装应用进行的处理。清除应用数据会重置首次提示，也会删除进度。此版本拒绝首次提示将退出应用；初始化后，没有在继续游戏的同时关闭所有广告服务处理的设置。Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics 关闭。
