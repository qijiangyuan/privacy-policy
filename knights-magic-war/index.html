Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how Riverstar Games (“we”, “us”) processes information when you use the Android game Knight’s Magic War (“Game”), package com.jcgame.materialMagic.global, and contact our support team.

1. Information we process

  • Account and sign-in: verified Google OpenID and Google Play Games player identifiers, temporary server authorization codes, internal player ID, selected server, and in-game profile information. Authorization codes are used to verify sign-in; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Gameplay: progress, heroes, troops, equipment, skills, inventory, virtual currency, rewards, battle results, in-game mail, settings, and related timestamps needed to operate and synchronize your game.
  • Purchases: when in-app purchases are available and you make a purchase, product and order identifiers, purchase token or receipt, purchase status, and the player and server receiving the item. Google Play processes payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase processes app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, language, app version, sessions, screen views, and interactions such as tutorials, battles, rewards, and virtual-currency use. It derives approximate location from masked IP addresses and may record purchase events such as product and price. Depending on device availability and collection settings, it can also collect the Android advertising ID. The Game does not show ads.
  • Service and support: network and security logs, including IP address, request time, and errors; and your email address, messages, or attachments when you contact us. Preferences and limited account or session data are also stored locally on your device.

2. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate players, maintain accounts, save progress, provide online gameplay, verify and deliver purchases, respond to support requests, understand feature use, measure the effectiveness of campaigns promoting the Game through Google Ads, improve reliability, detect fraud or cheating, and meet legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in operating and protecting it, legal obligations, or consent where required.

3. Providers and disclosures

We use Google Play Games Services for authentication, Google Play for distribution and payment processing, Google Analytics for Firebase for usage analysis, and hosting providers to operate our servers. Providers process relevant information to deliver these services. Google may also process information under its own terms and privacy practices.

We link Firebase Analytics with Google Ads to analyze campaign results and measure installations, purchases, and other in-game actions. Relevant analytics data, including app-instance or advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, gameplay events, and purchase events, may be shared with Google Ads for analysis and advertising measurement according to the linked-service settings. This association does not add advertising placements to the Game. We do not send your Google password, full payment-card details, or raw game-account credentials to Analytics or Google Ads.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. We may disclose necessary information to comply with law, protect players and the service, resolve disputes, or handle a business transfer with appropriate protections. We do not sell personal information for money or use it to display targeted advertising in the Game.

4. Retention

Account and gameplay data is kept while needed to provide your account and the Game. Support and technical records are kept as needed to resolve issues and protect the service; analytics data is subject to the configured retention settings of the analytics service. Limited transaction, security, dispute, or legal records may be retained longer where required by law or reasonably necessary for those purposes. We delete or de-identify data when it is no longer needed.

5. Deletion, choices, and rights

You can request deletion of your game account and associated data, or request deletion of specific information, by following our Account and Data Deletion Instructions. You do not need to reinstall the Game to make a request. Uninstalling the Game only removes local data and does not automatically delete server records.

You can manage Google permissions through your Google Account and advertising-ID controls through Android settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, copy, delete, restrict or object to processing of your information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may verify account ownership before acting.

6. Security and international processing

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information and limit access. No transmission or storage system is completely secure. Our servers and service providers may process information outside your country. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply to international transfers.

7. Younger players

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Where local law requires a higher age or parental permission for particular processing, those requirements apply. If you believe a child has provided information in violation of these requirements, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

8. This website and policy updates

This policy website does not embed analytics or advertising scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process technical access logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We may update this Policy as features or practices change, revise the effective date, and provide further notice where required.

9. Contact

Riverstar Games · qijiangyuan@gmail.com. Include the game name and enough information to locate your account. Do not send passwords, Google authorization codes, purchase tokens, full card details, or identity documents.

隐私政策（简体中文）

本政策说明 Riverstar Games（“我们”）在您使用 Android 游戏 Knight’s Magic War（骑士的魔法战争）（“本游戏”，包名 com.jcgame.materialMagic.global）及联系客服时如何处理信息。本游戏免费下载、提供可选内购、不展示广告，面向 13 岁及以上玩家。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 账户与登录：经验证的 Google OpenID 标识符、Google Play Games 玩家标识符、临时服务器授权码、内部玩家 ID、所选服务器和游戏内资料。授权码用于验证登录，我们不会收到您的 Google 密码。
  • 游戏数据：进度、英雄、部队、装备、技能、背包、虚拟货币、奖励、战斗结果、游戏内邮件、设置及运行和同步游戏所需的时间记录。
  • 购买数据：当内购功能开放且您进行购买时，我们会处理商品和订单标识符、购买令牌或收据、购买状态，以及接收商品的玩家和服务器。Google Play 处理付款信息，我们不会收到完整银行卡或银行账户信息。
  • 分析数据：Google Analytics for Firebase 处理应用实例标识符、设备和操作系统信息、语言、应用版本、会话、页面浏览，以及教程、战斗、奖励和虚拟货币使用等交互事件。它会通过经过掩码处理的 IP 地址推断大致位置，也可能记录商品和价格等购买事件。根据设备可用性与采集设置，它还可能收集 Android 广告 ID。本游戏不展示广告。
  • 服务与客服：包括 IP 地址、请求时间、错误在内的网络与安全日志；您联系客服时提供的邮箱、消息或附件。设备本地还会保存偏好设置以及少量账户或会话数据。

2. 信息用途

我们使用信息验证身份、维护账户、保存进度、提供在线游戏、验证购买并发货、处理客服请求、了解功能使用情况、衡量通过 Google Ads 推广本游戏的广告活动效果、改善稳定性、防范欺诈和作弊及履行法律义务。在需要明确处理依据的地区，我们依据提供您请求的服务、运营和保护服务的合法利益、法律义务，或在法律要求时取得的同意处理信息。

3. 服务商与披露

我们使用 Google Play Games Services 验证身份、Google Play 分发和处理付款、Google Analytics for Firebase 分析使用情况，并通过托管服务商运行服务器。服务商会处理提供相关服务所需的信息。Google 也可能按照自身条款和隐私规则处理信息。

我们将 Firebase Analytics 与 Google Ads 关联，以分析推广效果并衡量安装、购买及其他游戏内操作。应用实例或广告标识符、大致位置、应用互动、游戏事件和购买事件等相关分析数据，可能按照关联服务设置分享给 Google Ads，用于分析和广告效果衡量。此关联不会在本游戏中加入广告展示。我们不会将您的 Google 密码、完整银行卡信息或原始游戏账户凭据发送给 Analytics 或 Google Ads。

请参阅 Google 隐私政策及 Firebase 隐私说明。为遵守法律、保护玩家和服务、解决争议，或在采取适当保护措施的业务转让中，我们可能披露必要信息。我们不会以金钱为对价出售个人信息，也不会利用它在本游戏内展示定向广告。

4. 保存期限

我们会在提供账户及游戏服务所需期间保存账户和游戏数据。客服与技术记录按解决问题和保障服务所需期限保存；分析数据按照分析服务中配置的保留设置处理。法律要求或相关目的合理必要时，有限的交易、安全、争议或法律记录可能保留更长时间。信息不再需要时，我们会删除或去标识化处理。

5. 删除、选择与权利

您可以按照账户与数据删除说明申请删除游戏账户及关联数据，或仅申请删除特定信息。提交请求不要求重新安装游戏。卸载游戏只会删除本地数据，不会自动删除服务器记录。

您可以在 Google 账户中管理相关授权，在 Android 设置中管理广告 ID。根据所在地区，您可能有权访问、更正、复制、删除信息，限制或反对处理，撤回以同意为依据的处理授权，或向数据保护机构投诉。请联系我们行使适用权利；处理前我们可能核实账户归属。

6. 安全与跨境处理

我们采取合理措施保护信息并限制访问，但任何传输或存储系统都无法保证绝对安全。服务器及服务商可能在您所在国家或地区之外处理信息；法律要求时，跨境处理将适用相应保障措施。

7. 未成年玩家

本游戏面向 13 岁及以上玩家，不面向未满 13 岁的儿童。如果当地法律对特定数据处理要求更高年龄或监护人许可，应适用这些要求。如您认为儿童违反上述要求向我们提供了信息，请联系我们，我们会调查并在适当情况下删除。

本隐私政策网站未嵌入分析或广告脚本，使用 GitHub Pages 托管；托管商可能根据 GitHub 隐私声明处理访问日志。我们可能随功能或数据处理方式变化更新本政策，修改生效日期，并在需要时提供进一步通知。
