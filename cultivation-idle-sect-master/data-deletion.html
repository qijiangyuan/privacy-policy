Request deletion of your game account or data

This page applies to Cultivation: Idle Sect Master / 《我的宗门天下无敌》, provided by Riverstar Games (jcgame). Android package: com.jcgame.zongmenwudi.global.

You can request deletion without installing the Game or signing in to this website. To delete a game account or profile and associated online data, email us using the button below, or send an email directly to the displayed address.

Request account or data deletion
Contact: qijiangyuan@gmail.com

1. What to include in your request

  • Subject: Cultivation Idle Sect Master Account and Data Deletion.
  • Say whether you want your game account or profile and all associated online data deleted, only a cloud save or leaderboard record deleted, or another category such as support correspondence.
  • Include the game name, Android platform and, if known, your in-game player/profile ID or platform display name and an approximate date of use. For support correspondence, use the original contact email where possible.

If you cannot find your player ID or no longer have access to the Game, contact us anyway. We will explain what limited information is needed to locate records and verify that they belong to you. Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, purchase tokens or a complete save file.

2. Online account, cloud-save and leaderboard records

After proportionate verification, we delete the requested game-specific account or profile and associated data under our control, including linked cloud progress and leaderboard submissions where these features are used. We do not treat merely freezing an account as deletion. If a platform provider controls a record directly, we will explain its request route and assist where possible.

Deleting a game profile does not delete your Google account or all Google Play Games data for other games. Local progress, copies on another device and platform backups require the separate steps below. To avoid restoring old progress, also remove any cloud copy you want deleted before reconnecting or reinstalling.

3. Remove local saves

  1. Close the Game.
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Cultivation: Idle Sect Master → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data. On some devices the Game may appear as 我的宗门天下无敌.
  3. Confirm the action. Clearing storage removes app-local progress, settings and stored privacy choices. Clearing only the cache does not delete the save. Uninstalling also removes app-local data, subject to operating-system backup and restoration.

These actions can permanently remove local progress. We cannot remotely remove or recover a save that exists only on your device. Separately saved backups, copies on other devices and operating-system or cloud-provider backups must be managed through their own controls. Clearing local storage does not by itself delete online records.

4. Analytics, advertising and provider-held records

Firebase Analytics, Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Unity Ads and Google AdMob or regional consent services may hold usage, advertising, diagnostic or consent records. Clearing local storage, uninstalling, resetting an advertising ID or withdrawing consent does not automatically erase earlier provider-held records. You may request deletion of identifiable analytics data under our control using the contact route above. Stopping collection affects future events and does not itself delete earlier records.

We handle information under our control and work with providers processing data on our behalf. For information controlled independently by Google or Unity, use the request routes in the Google privacy policy and Unity player privacy policy. You can contact us for help. If you want help locating past SDK records, contact us before clearing or resetting the relevant identifiers where possible.

5. Purchases and subscription cancellation

Deleting an account or data and uninstalling the Game do not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel separately in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. A deletion request does not automatically provide a refund. Google may retain payment records for billing, tax, fraud prevention or legal requirements.

6. Timing and necessary retention

We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law. Following a verified request, we delete or anonymize covered information unless limited retention is necessary for a legal obligation, security, fraud prevention or resolving a dispute. Records no longer identifying you in aggregate may remain. If an exception applies, we explain which information is retained, why and for the applicable purpose and period. Providers apply their own processes and retention policies. See the Privacy Policy for further information.

申请删除账号或数据
联系邮箱：qijiangyuan@gmail.com

1. 申请需要说明的信息

  • 主题：Cultivation Idle Sect Master Account and Data Deletion／我的宗门天下无敌账号及数据删除。
  • 说明希望删除游戏账号或档案及全部关联在线数据、仅删除云存档或排行榜记录，还是客服往来等其他类别。
  • 注明游戏名、Android 平台，并在已知时提供游戏内玩家／档案 ID 或平台显示名称，以及大致使用日期。申请删除客服往来时，尽量使用原联系邮箱。

找不到玩家 ID 或已无法访问游戏时，仍可联系我们。我们会说明定位和核实记录所需的有限信息。请勿发送密码、银行卡资料、身份证件、购买令牌或完整存档。

2. 在线账号、云存档及排行榜记录

经与请求相称的核实后，我们会删除所申请的游戏专属账号或档案，以及由我们控制的关联数据，包括实际使用这些功能时关联的云端进度及排行榜成绩；不会以冻结账号替代删除。平台服务商直接控制记录时，我们会说明其申请渠道，并在可行时提供协助。

删除游戏档案不会删除您的 Google 账号，也不会删除其他游戏的全部 Google Play Games 数据。本地进度、其他设备上的副本及平台备份需按下方步骤另行处理。为避免恢复旧进度，请在重新连接或安装前一并删除您不希望保留的云端副本。

3. 删除本地存档

  1. 关闭游戏。
  2. 打开 Android 设置 → 应用 → Cultivation: Idle Sect Master／我的宗门天下无敌 → 存储与缓存 → 清除存储空间／清除数据。不同设备菜单名称可能不同。
  3. 确认操作。清除存储会移除应用本地进度、设置及已保存的隐私选择；仅“清除缓存”不会删除存档。卸载也会移除本地应用数据，并受操作系统备份及恢复行为影响。

这些操作可能永久删除本地进度。我们无法远程移除或恢复仅在您设备上的存档。另行保存的备份、其他设备副本、操作系统或云服务商备份需通过其各自控制管理。清除本地存储本身不会删除在线记录。

4. 统计、广告及服务商持有的记录

Firebase Analytics、Unity LevelPlay／ironSource、Unity Ads、Google AdMob 或地区同意服务可能持有使用、广告、诊断及同意记录。清除存储、卸载、重置广告 ID 或撤回同意，不会自动删除其此前持有的记录。您可通过上述联系方式，申请删除由我们控制的可识别统计数据。停止采集影响后续事件，不会自动删除此前记录。

我们会处理自己控制的信息，并与代表我们处理数据的服务商配合。Google 或 Unity 独立控制的信息，可通过 Google 隐私政策及 Unity 玩家隐私政策中的渠道申请，也可联系我们协助。需要定位历史 SDK 记录时，请尽可能在清除或重置相关标识前联系我们。

5. 购买及取消订阅

删除账号、数据或卸载游戏不会取消 Google Play 订阅。请在 Google Play → 付款与订阅 → 订阅中另行取消。删除申请不会自动退款；Google 可能为计费、税务、防欺诈或法律要求保留必要付款记录。

6. 处理期限及必要保留

我们会在适用法律要求的期限内回复。核实后，申请范围内的信息会被删除或匿名化；履行法律义务、安全、防欺诈或争议处理确需有限保留的除外。不再识别个人的汇总记录可能保留。适用例外时，我们会说明保留的信息、原因及必要用途和期间。服务商按各自流程及保留政策处理。详情见隐私政策。
