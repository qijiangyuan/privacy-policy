These identifiers may identify a device or link activity over time; they are not necessarily anonymous.
  • Analytics data: we use Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase Analytics) to understand game usage. The service may process an app instance identifier, device and app information, session information and usage events, such as game readiness and screens opened. Our custom analytics events do not include player names, contact emails, complete save files, order identifiers or purchase tokens.

  • Purchase data: product identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, transaction status and subscription entitlement or expiry information used to verify purchases, provide benefits and restore them. Google Play processes payments; we do not receive your payment card or bank details.

Where required by law, we rely on consent for relevant advertising and analytics activities. Local saves remain on your device until removed through game or Android storage controls. We retain support and service records only as long as needed for their stated purposes. After a valid deletion request, we delete or anonymize information under our control unless limited retention is necessary for payment or tax obligations, security, fraud prevention or resolving a dispute. Any necessary retention is limited to the applicable purpose and period; we explain an exception in our response. Providers apply their own retention policies. We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law.

We use reasonable safeguards and secure transport for online services. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. For practical steps and the scope of deletion, see account and data deletion.

Google Play handles billing, cancellation and payment records under its own terms and policies.
