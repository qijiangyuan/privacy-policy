This policy explains how Riverstar Games handles information in Twin Oaths: Three Kingdoms (the Game), Android package com.jcgame.twinoaths.global, and when you contact us or visit this policy website.

1. Information on your device

The Game stores campaign progress, recruited heroes, formations, gold, morale, battle results, reward claims and tutorial preferences locally on your device. Advertising-service acceptance is stored separately from your campaign. The Game has no player account, developer-operated cloud save, multiplayer, chat, online leaderboard or real-money in-app purchases. Virtual gold and rewards cannot be exchanged for money or real-world prizes.

Core gameplay works offline. Your complete save is not uploaded to a game server operated by us. The Android app disables standard app backup. Device manufacturers may still offer device-to-device transfer or other migration behavior outside the Game's control. Clearing app storage can remove local saves and preferences; review any device migration copies separately. See the Google Privacy Policy for platform services.

2. Optional advertising

The Android Game includes Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, plus integrated Ad Quality, for optional rewarded advertising, mediation and ad-quality monitoring. When you choose to enable advertising services, these services may process device and pseudonymous user identifiers, advertising and app-set identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, device and network characteristics, advertising requests and interactions, viewing and reward events, ad-performance and impression-revenue information, and technical diagnostics.

These data support ad delivery and measurement, reward operation, analytics, troubleshooting, quality monitoring, security and fraud prevention. Providers may share information with advertising partners and service providers under their policies. We may receive advertising performance and quality reports. A pseudonymous identifier is not necessarily anonymous.

The Game does not supply your real name, email address or a game-account identifier to the advertising services. It does not request access to precise location, contacts, camera or microphone. An approximate location may be inferred from an IP address without GPS access. Provider-created identifiers can exist even though the Game has no player accounts.

For provider practices and choices, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Unity Player Privacy FAQ and Unity Ads data disclosures.

3. Your advertising choices

You can play the ordinary game without enabling advertising. Selecting an ad reward before advertising is enabled opens a notice. Declining or dismissing that notice, or opening the policy, does not enable ads. Choosing to enable advertising stores your decision and allows initialization and preloading. After that choice, advertising services may initialize and preload on later launches even when you do not watch a particular reward ad.

The Game sends a negative personalized-ad consent signal and a do-not-sell signal through LevelPlay. Enabling the basic advertising service is not consent to personalized advertising. Contextual advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention may still process data.

Open Privacy & Ads in the Game to disable advertising without deleting campaign progress. This resets your advertising choice and stops new ad requests initiated by the Game. Restart the app to stop the advertising service that was already initialized. Disabling ads does not erase records already held by providers.

You can also use available Android advertising privacy settings and privacy controls supplied by an ad provider. Clearing the Game's storage resets its stored advertising choice, but also deletes local progress and preferences. Uninstalling stops future processing through that installation; it does not automatically delete provider records. See Data deletion for separate requests.

4. Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your support or privacy request. We keep support information only as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable legal, security or dispute-resolution requirements. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary identity documents.

Local progress remains until replaced or removed on your device; any device-migration copies must be managed separately. Advertising providers apply their own retention periods and deletion procedures. We can help identify the relevant request route, but we cannot erase a provider's records merely by resetting your game. You can request deletion of support correspondence that we hold.

5. Security, transfers and rights

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect access to your device and backups. Unity, platform, hosting and email providers may process information outside your country, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, withdraw consent, restrict or object to processing, opt out of sale or sharing, and complain to a relevant authority. Contact us for information we handle or use the provider's privacy channel for information it independently controls. Limited verification may be necessary to locate and protect your records.

6. Age range

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Store content ratings are separate from privacy consent requirements. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to us without required authorization so that we can investigate.

7. Other services and this website

The Game does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Hero illustrations and other artwork are bundled with the app; the Game does not send your prompts or images to an online AI service during play.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It adds no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, remote fonts or submission forms. GitHub may process technical request data to operate and secure hosting. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
