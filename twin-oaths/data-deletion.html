Privacy policy

The Game has no player account or developer cloud save. Local progress, support messages and advertising-provider records have different deletion routes.

Delete local game data

  1. Open Android Settings and find Apps.
  2. Select Twin Oaths: Three Kingdoms.
  3. Open Storage (or Storage & cache) and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device.

This removes the local campaign, heroes, formation, gold, morale, reward claims, tutorial preferences and advertising-service choice. This action can permanently remove progress. Starting a new campaign only replaces campaign progress; it does not delete all app preferences or provider records. Uninstalling can also remove local data. The app disables standard Android app backup, but manufacturer device-to-device transfer behavior can vary. Review any migration copies separately.

Request deletion of support information

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject Twin Oaths Data Deletion. Tell us which email address or correspondence the request concerns. No game login is needed. We may ask for limited verification and will explain any applicable legal, security or dispute-related retention requirement. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Request deletion of advertising data

Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Ad Quality may hold records linked to advertising or other pseudonymous identifiers after you have enabled ads. Clearing the Game or uninstalling does not itself remove these records. Use the request options in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and the privacy controls available inside a Unity ad. The Unity privacy FAQ explains provider data and choices.

You can also email Riverstar Games at the address above for help locating the relevant provider process. We cannot guarantee deletion of records controlled independently by a provider or remove legally required retention on its behalf. A provider may need an advertising or other identifier to locate records; provide it only through the provider's appropriate request channel. Android advertising-ID deletion or reset does not by itself erase existing provider records.

Stop future use through the Game

You may decline the advertising notice and continue ordinary play. If you previously enabled ads, open Privacy & Ads and disable advertising. This resets the stored choice and stops new ad requests initiated by the Game, without deleting your campaign. Restart the app to stop the already-initialized advertising service. This does not erase existing provider records.

Clearing app storage also resets the advertising choice but removes local progress; uninstalling stops processing through that installation. Available device and in-ad privacy settings offer additional controls described by their providers.

For details of the data involved, retention and privacy rights, read the privacy policy.
