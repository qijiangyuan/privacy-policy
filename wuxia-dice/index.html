English

This Privacy Policy explains how jcgame (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Wuxia Dice: Roguelike RPG, also known as Wuxia Dice and 骰定江湖 (the “Game”), Android package com.jcgame.wuxiadice.global, and when you contact us or visit this policy website.

The Game saves progress on your device and does not require a player account or provide developer-operated cloud saves. It uses Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads for optional rewarded ads. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled in the current version, 0.11.3. This policy distinguishes the current release from services that may be enabled in a later update.

1. Information handled

Local game data. Run progress, characters, equipment, skills, achievements, local records, previously claimed rewards, and your language preference are stored on your device. English is the default; you can choose Simplified Chinese in the main menu or game settings. We do not receive your complete saves or language preference through a jcgame game server. The current version has no player-account registration, online leaderboard, player chat, or developer-operated cloud synchronization.

Advertising and technical information. Unity LevelPlay (including ironSource services), Unity Ads, and the advertising partners used to serve ads may process advertising or other app/device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics. This may include device model, operating system, language, app version, network information, ad requests, impressions, clicks, viewing completion, and reward-related events. Processing depends on the service, configuration, device, region, and applicable privacy choices.

Privacy preferences. The Game records your acceptance of its startup privacy notice on the device. It sends privacy signals to its advertising services as described in Section 3.

Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or information you choose to provide. We use these to respond and handle support or privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, government identification, or unnecessary sensitive information.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, SMS, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location. Approximate location inferred from an IP address is different from precise device location.

2. Purposes of processing

Local information lets the Game run, remember progress and stored privacy choices, and prevent repeated reward claims. Advertising services use relevant information for ad delivery and measurement, reward verification, troubleshooting, security, and fraud prevention. Support information is used to answer requests, investigate issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable, processing may rely on providing a requested service, legitimate interests in support and security, legal obligations, or consent where required. Third-party providers describe their own processing and legal bases in their policies. Reading or accepting this policy is not, by itself, consent to personalized advertising.

3. Advertising and your choices

The present version displays a startup privacy notice in English or Simplified Chinese. The full policy is available from that notice, the main menu, and game settings. Selecting a language or opening the policy does not count as acceptance. Accepting the notice records your choice and allows you to enter the Game; declining it closes the Game. After acceptance, the Game initializes its configured advertising services and may preload ads before you select a reward offer.

Rewarded ads are optional once you enter the Game: you may decline an offer and continue playing without that offer's reward. Declining a rewarded ad does not itself undo startup acceptance or stop all advertising-service processing.

The current version sends signals indicating that personalized-ad consent has not been granted and that sale/sharing is opted out of through LevelPlay's supported privacy settings. These signals do not mean that advertising services process no information: delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention may still require data, subject to applicable law.

You can manage available advertising-identifier and ad privacy controls in Android settings. To reset the Game's stored startup acceptance, clear its app storage; the next launch will display the startup notice again. Clearing storage also removes local game progress. Uninstalling the Game stops further processing through the installed app, but does not automatically delete information already held by a provider. Contact us or use the relevant provider's privacy channels for additional rights or deletion requests.

4. Service providers and disclosures

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads: advertising mediation, delivery, reporting, and fraud prevention. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: disabled in the current release. If enabled in a later version, these services may process app-instance or installation identifiers, device/app information, usage and ad events, and crash diagnostics for analytics and stability. We will update this policy and provide applicable notices and choices before enabling that processing. See Firebase's privacy and security information.
  • Android / Google Play and device backup services: installation, distribution, and any device backup or restore features you use are governed by the platform's settings and policies, including Google's Privacy Policy where applicable.
  • Email services: delivery and storage of messages sent to our support address.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising-related processing may fall within broader legal definitions of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising in some jurisdictions. Providers may disclose information to their service providers or advertising partners as explained in their policies and subject to applicable choices. We may disclose support information where legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

5. Retention and deletion

Local saves and startup acceptance records remain until removed from the device. Use Android's Settings → Apps → Wuxia Dice / 骰定江湖 → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the Game, to remove app-local information. Labels vary by device. We cannot remotely access or erase your device-only saves.

The Android app allows system backup. Depending on your device and platform settings, backups may retain copies or restore data after reinstallation. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not necessarily delete those backups; review the relevant backup settings separately.

We retain support communications for as long as reasonably needed to handle requests and meet applicable legal, security, or dispute-resolution requirements. Advertising providers apply their own retention practices. Removing local data does not delete their historical records. For instructions and request routes, see Data Deletion.

6. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, including limiting access to support communications and service accounts. Please protect your device and backups. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Unity, Google, GitHub, email providers, and other relevant service providers may process information outside your country or region. Their policies describe their processing locations, applicable transfer safeguards, and privacy request channels. Where we are responsible for a transfer, we apply the protections required by applicable law.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13, or below an applicable local age threshold. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion or protective steps. A store content rating does not constitute consent to processing personal information.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information; withdraw consent; opt out of certain sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising processing; and complain to a data-protection authority.

Contact us about information we hold or process through our service providers. We may request limited information needed to verify and locate your request, and will respond within applicable legal time limits. For data a provider controls independently, use its own privacy request channels; we can help identify the relevant provider. You will not be penalized for exercising applicable privacy rights.

9. This policy website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or remote fonts to them. The Game's advertising services are separate from this website. GitHub may process technical request information, including visitors' IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Updates and contact

We will revise this policy and its date when our practices change, and provide further notice or request consent where required. For privacy questions or requests, contact jcgame at qijiangyuan@gmail.com, with Wuxia Dice Privacy in the subject.

隐私偏好：游戏在设备上记录您对启动隐私提示的接受情况，并按第三节所述向广告服务传达隐私信号。

客服通信：如果您发送邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容以及您主动提供的附件或信息，用于回复并处理客服或隐私请求。请勿发送密码、银行卡信息、身份证件或不必要的敏感信息。

游戏不申请访问您的通讯录、短信、相机、麦克风或精确 GPS 位置。通过 IP 地址推断的大致位置与设备的精确位置不同。

本地信息用于运行游戏、记住进度和保存的隐私选择，以及避免重复领取奖励。广告服务使用相关信息投放和衡量广告、核验奖励、排查问题、维护安全及防止欺诈。客服信息用于回复请求、调查问题和履行适用的法律义务。

在适用情况下，处理可能基于提供您请求的服务、客服与安全方面的合法利益、法律义务，以及依法需要的同意。第三方服务商在其政策中说明自己的处理方式和依据。阅读或接受本政策本身不代表同意个性化广告。
