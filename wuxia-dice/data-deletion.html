These instructions apply to Wuxia Dice: Roguelike RPG / 骰定江湖, Android package com.jcgame.wuxiadice.global, provided by jcgame.

The Game has no player account to delete. Removing device-local information and requesting deletion of information held by us or a service provider are separate steps.

1. Remove local game data and reset startup acceptance

  1. Close the Game.
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Wuxia Dice / 骰定江湖 → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. Names vary by device and language.
  3. Confirm the removal. You may also uninstall the Game to remove its app-local information.

This removes local progress, characters, equipment, achievements, reward-claim records, and stored startup privacy acceptance. Your progress may be permanently lost. After clearing storage, the next launch displays the startup privacy notice again. We cannot remotely erase information stored only on your device.

Device or platform backups may remain and may restore data after reinstallation. Review and remove relevant backup copies through those services' settings if needed. Clearing local data does not itself delete third-party online records.

2. Request deletion from jcgame

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject Wuxia Dice Data Deletion. Describe the information you want removed, approximately when you used the relevant service, and the email address used in any earlier support correspondence.

We may request limited information needed to verify and locate the request. Please do not send passwords, government identification, payment-card details, a complete game save, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We handle valid requests for personal information we hold or process through our service providers, including support communications. If Firebase is enabled in a future version and identifiable data is processed on our behalf, we will use the relevant provider tools where applicable. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled in the current 0.11.2 release.

3. Advertising-provider information and future processing

Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads may hold information from advertising services. Uninstalling, clearing storage, resetting an advertising identifier, or changing a privacy preference does not automatically erase their existing records.

Contact us about information processed on our behalf. For information controlled independently by Unity, use the rights and contact channels in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. For future enabled Firebase services, see Firebase's privacy information. We can help identify the appropriate route.

Android advertising settings can help manage available controls for future processing. Resetting the Game's startup acceptance requires clearing app storage as described above; declining the next startup notice closes the Game. Deletion requests and controls for future processing are separate.

4. Response and necessary retention

We respond within applicable legal time limits. Information covered by a valid request will be deleted or otherwise handled as required, except where limited retention is needed for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or resolving disputes. We will explain any applicable exception or information needed to locate a record. Provider-controlled information is handled through the relevant provider's own process.

简体中文 · 数据删除

本说明适用于 jcgame 提供的《骰定江湖》／Wuxia Dice: Roguelike RPG，Android 包名为 com.jcgame.wuxiadice.global。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日。

游戏没有需要注销的玩家账号。删除设备本地信息，与申请删除我们或服务商持有的信息，是两个不同的步骤。

1. 删除本地游戏数据并重置启动接受记录

  1. 关闭游戏。
  2. 打开 Android 设置 → 应用 → Wuxia Dice／骰定江湖 → 存储 → 清除存储空间／清除数据。不同设备和语言的名称可能不同。
  3. 确认清除。您也可以卸载游戏，以移除应用本地信息。

这会移除本地进度、角色、装备、成就、奖励领取记录和保存的启动隐私接受记录。您的进度可能永久丢失。清除存储后，下次启动会再次显示启动隐私提示。我们无法远程清除仅存于您设备上的信息。

设备或平台备份可能仍然保留，并可能在重新安装后恢复数据。如有需要，请通过相应服务的设置查看和删除有关备份副本。删除本地信息本身不会删除第三方在线记录。

2. 向 jcgame 申请删除

请发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，主题注明“Wuxia Dice Data Deletion”或“骰定江湖数据删除”。请说明希望删除的信息、大致使用有关服务的时间，以及曾联系客户支持时使用的邮箱。

我们可能要求核实和定位请求所需的有限信息。请勿发送密码、身份证件、银行卡信息、完整游戏存档或不必要的敏感信息。

我们会处理涉及我们持有或通过服务商处理的个人信息的有效请求，包括客服通信。如果后续版本启用 Firebase，且相关数据可被定位并代表我们处理，我们会在适用情况下使用相关服务商的工具。当前 0.11.2 版本关闭 Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics。

3. 广告服务商信息与后续处理

Unity LevelPlay／ironSource 和 Unity Ads 可能持有广告服务产生的信息。卸载、清除存储、重置广告标识符或修改隐私偏好，不会自动删除其既有记录。

涉及代表我们处理的信息，请联系我们。由 Unity 独立控制的信息，请使用 Unity 游戏玩家及应用用户隐私政策中的权利和联系渠道。后续启用的 Firebase 服务可参阅 Firebase 隐私说明。我们可以协助识别适用渠道。

Android 广告设置可以帮助管理后续处理的可用控制。重置游戏的启动接受记录需要按前述步骤清除应用存储；下次启动时拒绝隐私提示会退出游戏。数据删除请求与管理后续处理的控制相互独立。

4. 回复与必要保留

我们会在适用法律要求的期限内回复。有效请求涉及的信息将被删除或依法作其他处理；为履行法律义务、维护安全、防止欺诈或解决争议所需的有限保留除外。我们会说明适用例外或定位记录所需的信息。由服务商独立控制的信息按相关服务商自己的流程处理。
