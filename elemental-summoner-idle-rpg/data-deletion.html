Delete Your Game Data

Effective and last updated: August 22, 2026

This page explains how users of Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG (Android package com.jcgame.yuanshizhaohuan.global) can delete local game data or request deletion of other information associated with the Game.

The current release does not create a jcgame account and does not upload saved progress to a jcgame cloud server. Clearing storage or uninstalling permanently removes the local save from that device and we cannot restore it.
1

Delete local game data

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Select Apps, then Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG.
  3. Select Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage or Clear data.

Uninstalling the Game also deletes its local game data.

2

Send a deletion request

Email us with the subject Data Deletion Request – Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG. State that you use Android and describe the information you want deleted.

Email a deletion request

qijiangyuan@gmail.com

Information to include

  • The Game name: Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG;
  • Your platform: Android;
  • The type of request, such as deleting advertising-related or support-related information;
  • Only if we later request it, limited device or advertising information needed to locate a relevant record.

Do not email passwords, payment-card details, bank information, government identity documents, or a complete advertising identifier unless specifically requested. We may need to verify that a request concerns you. We normally respond within 30 days or the period required by applicable law.

What is deleted or may be retained

  • Clearing app data or uninstalling removes locally stored progress, virtual items and currency, settings, daily state, and rewarded-ad state from that device.
  • The Game has no jcgame account or server-side cloud save to delete.
  • Following a verified request, we delete or de-identify personal information under our control that is not required to be retained.
  • We may keep deletion-request correspondence for up to 90 days after closure, unless security, a dispute, or law requires longer retention.
  • Aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer identify you may be retained.

Third-party advertising data

Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and Google AdMob independently control some advertising records. You can manage advertising privacy and reset or delete the advertising ID through Android or Google settings where available. A provider may need to handle a request about its records directly.

删除您的游戏数据

生效及最近更新日期：2026 年 8 月 22 日

本页面说明 Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG（Android 软件包名称： com.jcgame.yuanshizhaohuan.global）用户如何删除本地游戏数据，或请求 删除与本游戏有关的其他信息。

当前版本不会创建 jcgame 账号，也不会将游戏存档上传至 jcgame 云服务器。 清除存储或卸载会永久删除该设备上的本地存档，我们无法为您恢复。
1

删除本地游戏数据

  1. 打开 Android 设置；
  2. 选择应用，然后选择 Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG；
  3. 选择存储和缓存；
  4. 点击清除存储空间或清除数据。

卸载本游戏也会删除本地游戏数据。

2

发送删除请求

请以 Data Deletion Request – Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG 为主题 向我们发送邮件，说明您使用 Android，并描述希望删除的信息。

发送删除请求邮件

qijiangyuan@gmail.com

邮件中需要提供的信息

  • 游戏名称：Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG；
  • 使用平台：Android；
  • 请求类型，例如删除广告相关或客服相关信息；
  • 仅在我们随后要求时，提供查找相关记录所需的有限设备或广告信息。

请勿通过邮件发送密码、支付卡信息、银行信息、政府身份证件或完整广告标识符，除非我们明确要求。 处理请求前，我们可能需要核实该请求确实与您有关。我们通常会在 30 天内或适用法律规定的期限内回复。

将删除或可能保留的信息

  • 清除应用数据或卸载本游戏，会从该设备删除本地游戏进度、虚拟物品与货币、设置、每日状态和激励广告状态；
  • 本游戏没有需要删除的 jcgame 账号或服务器端云存档；
  • 收到并核实请求后，我们会删除或去标识化由我们控制且无须继续保留的个人信息；
  • 请求关闭后，我们可能保留删除请求往来记录最多 90 天；安全、争议或法律要求更长期限的除外；
  • 无法再识别您的汇总或去标识化信息可能会继续保留。
