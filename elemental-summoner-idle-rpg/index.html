Privacy Policy (English)

Effective and last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how jcgame ("we," "us," or "our") processes information when you use the Android game Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG (the "Game").

1. Information We Process

Information stored locally. The Game stores progress, summoned characters, equipment, virtual items and currency, rewards, unlocks, statistics, daily check-in state, language, audio preferences, tutorial state, rewarded-ad limits and cooldowns, and related settings in the Game's local app storage. We do not operate a jcgame player-account system, game backend, or cloud-save service for the current release, and we do not receive your local save data.

Your device or operating-system provider may include app data in a device backup if backups are enabled. The Game disables Android application backup, but device or platform behavior may change. Any platform backup is controlled by the relevant provider and your account settings, not by jcgame.

Advertising information. The Game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation to provide optional rewarded advertisements. LevelPlay and the advertising partner selected for an ad request, including Google AdMob or Unity Ads, may process information such as:

  • the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or app identifiers where available;
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language, app and SDK versions, screen characteristics, network type, and mobile carrier;
  • ad requests, impressions, clicks, video completion, rewards, and other interactions with advertisements;
  • diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information.

The advertising SDK may initialize and load an advertisement when the Game starts, even if you do not later choose to view a rewarded advertisement.

The information actually processed depends on your region, device, privacy choices, the provider serving the ad, and whether an ad is available. Advertising providers may combine this information with information they already hold as described in their own policies.

Firebase Analytics. Firebase Analytics libraries are included in the Android build, but automatic collection and the Game's Firebase event reporting are disabled in the current release.

Information you send to us. If you contact us, we process your email address, message, attachments, and any game or device information you choose to provide so that we can respond and investigate your request.

Information we do not request. The current release does not require a name, postal address, phone number, email address, or jcgame account to play. It does not provide cloud saves, Google Play Games sign-in, purchases, or subscriptions. The Game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, videos, camera, or microphone.

2. How and Why Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to save progress, remember settings, enforce rewarded-ad limits, and provide the Game's features. Advertising providers may use advertising information to request, select, deliver, measure, limit, and secure advertisements; detect invalid traffic and fraud; produce reporting; comply with law; and, where permitted by your choices and applicable law, personalize advertising. We use support information to answer requests, troubleshoot problems, protect the Game, and comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on your consent, performance of our agreement with you, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Game, or compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent where the relevant provider or platform makes that choice available, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

3. Third-Party Services and Disclosure

Advertising information may be transmitted directly from your device to the provider serving an advertisement. The current advertising stack includes:

  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, for ad mediation, delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention;
  • Google AdMob, for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention;
  • Google Play, for app distribution and platform services.

These providers may act as independent controllers for some processing and may process data in countries other than yours. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and notices. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and Google's advertising technologies information.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the Game, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the Game. jcgame does not sell personal information for money. Advertising providers' use of identifiers for personalized or cross-context advertising may be treated as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under some laws, subject to applicable consent and opt-out rights.

4. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

  • You can choose whether to request an optional rewarded advertisement.
  • You can manage advertising privacy and reset or delete the advertising ID through Android or Google settings, where available.
  • You can clear the Game's local data through Android settings or uninstall the Game.
  • You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, or an appeal where the law where you live provides that right.

Because we do not maintain a jcgame account or receive local save data, we may not hold information that we can associate with you. A request about information independently controlled by Unity or Google may need to be submitted directly to that provider. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the Game, or the operating system removes it. Advertising providers determine their own retention periods under their policies. Support correspondence is normally kept only while needed to respond and for up to 90 days after the request is closed, unless a longer period is reasonably needed for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer identify you may be retained.

6. Security and International Processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information under our control. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Advertising and platform providers may process information outside your country. Where required, those transfers are subject to safeguards described by the relevant provider.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by local law to consent to data processing. We do not knowingly ask children below that age to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game or to our support address, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the Game, our practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here, update the effective date, and provide additional notice where required.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email qijiangyuan@gmail.com. Include the Game name and a description of your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identity documents, or full advertising identifiers unless we specifically request limited information needed to locate a record.

隐私政策（简体中文）

生效及最近更新日期：2026 年 8 月 22 日

本隐私政策说明 jcgame（以下简称“我们”）在您使用 Android 游戏 Elemental Summoner: Idle RPG（以下简称“本游戏”）时如何处理信息。

1. 我们处理的信息

保存在本地的信息。本游戏会将游戏进度、已召唤角色、装备、虚拟物品与货币、 奖励、解锁内容、统计数据、每日签到状态、语言、音频偏好、教程状态、激励广告次数与冷却时间及 相关设置保存在应用的本地存储中。当前版本不提供 jcgame 玩家账号系统、游戏后端或云存档服务， 我们不会收到您的本地存档数据。

如果设备备份已开启，您的设备或操作系统服务商可能将应用数据纳入备份。本游戏已关闭 Android 应用备份，但设备或平台行为可能发生变化。平台备份由相关服务商及您的账号设置控制，并非由 jcgame 控制。

广告相关信息。本游戏使用 Unity LevelPlay 聚合提供可选激励广告。Unity LevelPlay 以及为某次广告请求提供服务的广告合作方（包括 Google AdMob 或 Unity Ads）可能处理：

  • Android 广告 ID、应用集 ID，以及设备支持时的其他设备或应用标识符；
  • IP 地址及由其推断的大致位置；
  • 设备型号、操作系统、语言、应用及 SDK 版本、屏幕特征、网络类型和移动运营商；
  • 广告请求、展示、点击、视频完成、奖励及其他广告互动；
  • 诊断、性能、安全和防止欺诈所需的信息。

广告 SDK 可能会在本游戏启动时初始化并加载广告，即使您之后没有选择观看激励广告。

实际处理的信息取决于您所在地区、设备、隐私选择、提供广告的服务商以及广告是否可用。广告 服务商可能按照其自身政策，将这些信息与其已经持有的信息结合使用。

Firebase Analytics。Android 构建中包含 Firebase Analytics 程序库， 但当前版本已关闭自动采集及本游戏的 Firebase 事件上报。

您主动发送的信息。当您联系我们时，我们会处理您的邮箱地址、消息、附件，以及 您自愿提供的游戏或设备信息，以回复并调查您的请求。

我们不会请求的信息。当前版本无需提供姓名、邮寄地址、电话号码、电子邮箱或创建 jcgame 账号即可游玩；不提供云存档、Google Play Games 登录、内购或订阅。本游戏不会请求访问 精确位置、联系人、照片、视频、相机或麦克风。

2. 信息的用途及处理原因

本地信息用于保存进度、记住设置、执行激励广告次数限制和提供游戏功能。广告服务商可能使用广告 信息来请求、选择、投放、衡量、限制和保护广告，识别无效流量和欺诈，生成报告，履行法律义务， 并在您的选择及适用法律允许时提供个性化广告。我们使用客服信息来回复请求、排查问题、保护本游戏 以及履行法律义务。

在适用法律要求说明处理依据的地区，处理可能基于您的同意、履行我们与您的协议、运营和保护本游戏 的合法利益或履行法律义务。在相关服务商或平台提供相应选项时，您可以撤回同意；撤回不影响此前 基于同意进行处理的合法性。

3. 第三方服务及信息披露

广告信息可能由您的设备直接传输给提供广告的服务商。当前广告服务包括：

  • Unity LevelPlay 和 Unity Ads：用于广告聚合、投放、衡量及防止欺诈；
  • Google AdMob：用于广告投放、衡量及防止欺诈；
  • Google Play：用于应用分发和平台服务。

对于部分处理活动，上述服务商可能作为独立的数据控制者，并可能在您所在国家以外处理数据。其 信息处理受各自条款及隐私声明约束。请参阅 Unity 游戏玩家和应用用户隐私政策、 Google 隐私权政策及 Google 广告技术说明。

在法律要求、保护用户或本游戏，或本游戏发生合并、收购、融资或转让时，我们也可能披露必要信息。 jcgame 不会以金钱为对价出售个人信息。广告服务商使用标识符提供个性化广告或跨情境广告的行为， 在部分法律下可能被视为“出售”“共享”或定向广告，并受相应的同意及退出权约束。

4. 您的选择和隐私权利

  • 您可以自行选择是否请求观看可选激励广告；
  • 在设备支持时，您可以通过 Android 或 Google 设置管理广告隐私，并重置或删除广告 ID；
  • 您可以通过 Android 设置清除本游戏的本地数据，或卸载本游戏；
  • 如果您所在地区的法律赋予相关权利，您可以联系我们请求访问、更正、删除、限制或反对处理、数据可携带、撤回同意或提出申诉。

由于我们不维护 jcgame 账号，也不会收到本地存档，因此我们可能不持有能够与您关联的信息。对于 Unity 或 Google 独立控制的信息，您可能需要直接向相应服务商提交请求。请参阅 数据删除说明。

5. 保存与删除

本地游戏数据会保存在您的设备上，直至您清除应用存储、卸载本游戏或操作系统将其删除。广告服务商 会根据各自政策确定保存期限。客服往来通常仅在处理请求期间以及请求结束后最多 90 天内保存；为安全、 争议解决或合规而合理需要更长期限的除外。无法再识别您的汇总或去标识化信息可能会继续保留。

6. 安全与跨境处理

我们采取合理的管理和技术措施保护由我们控制的信息，但任何存储或传输系统均无法保证绝对安全。 广告及平台服务商可能在您所在国家以外处理信息；在法律要求时，相关跨境传输将采用服务商所述的保障措施。

7. 儿童隐私

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，或当地法律规定的可自行同意数据处理最低年龄以下的儿童。我们不会故意 要求低于该年龄的儿童提供个人信息。如果您认为儿童通过本游戏或客服邮箱提供了个人信息，请联系我们， 以便我们调查并在适当情况下删除。

8. 本政策的变更

当本游戏、我们的做法、第三方服务或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。我们会在此发布修订 版本、更新生效日期，并在法律要求时另行通知。

9. 联系我们

如有隐私问题或请求，请发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，并注明游戏名称及请求内容。 请勿发送密码、完整支付卡信息、政府身份证件或完整广告标识符；仅在我们明确要求查找记录所需的有限 信息时再提供。
