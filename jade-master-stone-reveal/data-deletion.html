Jade Master: Stone Reveal does not use player accounts or store game progress on jcgame servers. Your progress and preferences are stored in the Game’s local app storage.

Delete Local Game Data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Jade Master: Stone Reveal → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Menu names can vary by device. You can also uninstall the Game. Either action removes locally stored progress and settings from that device.

Device Backups

If Android backup is enabled, your device or operating-system provider may retain a backup and may restore app data later. Review or delete backups through your Android and Google account backup settings. These backups are not stored on or controlled by jcgame servers.

Advertising Data and Privacy Choices

Advertising and consent information may be processed independently by Google. When the option is required and available, open the Game’s Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy to review your advertising privacy choices. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings and review Google’s Privacy Policy.
