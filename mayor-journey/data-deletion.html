Privacy Policy简体中文

Local progress and preferences

The Game has no player account or developer-operated cloud save. To remove local app data, open Android Settings, choose Apps, find Mayor Journey City Builder, then choose its Storage settings and Clear storage or Clear data. Names vary by device. This removes city progress, stories, local reward records, language/audio preferences and advertising-service acceptance. Uninstalling may also remove local data. These actions can permanently remove progress.

Android backups or device migration may retain or restore a separate copy. Review your device and Google backup settings separately. The game menu's restart or abandon option concerns the current game save and is not a complete removal of all settings or provider records.

Advertising-provider information

Clearing app storage, declining an ad or uninstalling does not automatically delete records already held by Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads or Ad Quality. Use the request channels described in Unity Player Privacy Policy and Unity Player Privacy FAQ. Providers may need limited identifying information to locate a request. Please follow their secure instructions rather than emailing passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Support and privacy requests

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with subject Mayor Journey Data Deletion. Describe the information or correspondence you want removed. Riverstar Games can address information it holds and help identify the relevant provider. Advertising providers apply their own retention and deletion processes. Information may be retained where needed for applicable legal, security or dispute-resolution obligations. We will respond according to applicable law.

本地进度与偏好

游戏没有玩家账号或开发者云存档。删除本地应用数据时，可在 Android 设置中进入应用，找到《我来当市长》／Mayor Journey City Builder，再进入存储并选择清除存储或清除数据。名称依设备不同。此操作会移除城市、剧情、奖励记录、语言与音量偏好及广告接受记录，卸载也可能删除本地数据。这些操作可能永久丢失进度。

系统备份或设备迁移可能保留或恢复另一份数据，请另行检查设备及 Google 备份设置。游戏菜单的重新开始或放弃只处理当前存档，不等于清除全部设置或供应商记录。

广告供应商数据

清除应用数据、拒绝一次广告或卸载不会自动删除 Unity LevelPlay、Unity Ads 或 Ad Quality 已持有的记录。请使用 Unity 玩家隐私政策及 隐私常见问题提供的渠道。供应商可能要求有限定位信息，请按其安全途径操作，不要邮件发送密码或不必要的身份证明。

客服与隐私请求

发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，主题注明 Mayor Journey Data Deletion，说明希望删除的信息或通信记录。Riverstar Games 可处理自己持有的信息并协助识别相关供应商；供应商执行自己的保留及删除流程。法律、安全或争议解决义务要求的信息可能继续保留，我们依适用法律回复。
