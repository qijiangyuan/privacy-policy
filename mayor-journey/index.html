English

This policy explains how Riverstar Games, using the jcgame app namespace, handles information in Mayor Journey City Builder, also called 穿越成为祁厅长：我来当市长 (the Game), Android package com.jcgame.shizhangmoniqi, and when you contact us or visit this policy website.

1. Information stored on your device

City layouts, budgets, buildings, projects, career and story progress, game choices and reward-claim records are saved locally. Language and audio settings and advertising-service acceptance are also stored on your device. English is the default language and Simplified Chinese is available from the main menu or game settings. The Game has no player account, developer-operated cloud save, player chat, online leaderboard or real-money in-app purchases.

The Game does not send your complete save to our game server. Android backup or device migration may copy or restore app data depending on platform and device settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove local progress but does not necessarily delete a platform backup. See Google Privacy Policy for relevant platform services.

2. Advertising services and information

The current Android version includes Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and integrated Ad Quality for rewarded advertising, mediation and ad-quality monitoring. These services may process advertising identifiers, App Set IDs, installation and other pseudonymous user or device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and operating-system information, app and network information, ad requests, impressions, clicks, viewing completion, reward events, advertising performance and impression revenue, ad-content metadata and technical diagnostics.

This information supports advertising delivery and measurement, reward operation, quality monitoring, analytics, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Providers may disclose information to their service providers and advertising partners under their policies. Advertising dashboards may give us performance and quality reports. Pseudonymous information is not necessarily anonymous.

The Game does not provide your real name, email address or a custom player-account ID to the ad SDKs. It does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. An IP-derived approximate location does not require a GPS permission. Integrated services may use their own identifiers and technical startup components even though the Game has no account.

See Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Unity Player Privacy FAQ and LevelPlay data disclosures.

3. Advertising choices

Core single-player play works without watching an ad. When advertising services have not been enabled, selecting an ad reward opens a notice. Choosing Not now, dismissing the notice or opening this policy does not enable rewarded ads and you can keep playing. Choosing Enable stores acceptance and allows the Game to initialize and preload advertising. Once enabled, services may initialize and preload again on later launches. Declining an individual reward offer does not stop every advertising-service data operation.

The Game currently sends LevelPlay a negative GDPR personalized-ad consent signal and a do-not-sell signal. Acceptance of the basic service notice is not consent to personalized advertising. These settings do not mean that contextual advertising, measurement, quality monitoring or security processing stops. Provider processing is subject to applicable law and available privacy controls.

Use available Android advertising-identifier and ad privacy settings and privacy options shown by an ad provider. Clearing the Game's app storage resets its stored acceptance, but also removes local game progress and preferences. Uninstalling stops further processing through the installed Game, but does not automatically remove provider records. Contact us or use the provider privacy channels for requests about those records. See Data Deletion.

4. Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer support or privacy requests and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose and applicable legal, security or dispute-resolution requirements. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents.

Local saves remain until replaced or removed on your device, subject to platform backup behavior. Advertising providers apply their own retention practices. You may contact us for assistance identifying the relevant provider or request route. Removing a local save does not delete information already held by an advertising provider.

5. Security, transfers and privacy rights

We apply reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. Protect your device and backups. No storage or transmission method guarantees complete security. Unity, platform, hosting and email providers may process information outside your country under their policies and applicable transfer protections.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict or object to processing of your information, withdraw consent, opt out of sale or sharing and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us about information we handle or use the provider channels for information that a provider independently controls. We may request limited information needed to locate and verify a request. Exercising applicable privacy rights will not cause a penalty.

6. Age range

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. The appropriate store content rating is separate from privacy consent. If you believe a child provided personal information to us without required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

7. Other services and this website

The current Game does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. If we add analytics, accounts, purchases or other services, we will update the policy and relevant disclosures. Game story artwork is included in the app and is not generated by an online AI service while you play.

This policy website uses GitHub Pages hosting and does not add advertising, analytics scripts, forms, tracking pixels or remote fonts. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the site. See GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Contact and updates

Contact Riverstar Games at qijiangyuan@gmail.com with Mayor Journey Privacy in the subject. We will update this page and its date when practices change and provide additional notices or choices where required.

简体中文 · 隐私政策

本政策说明 Riverstar Games（应用命名空间为 jcgame）在《穿越成为祁厅长：我来当市长》／Mayor Journey City Builder（以下简称游戏，Android 包名 com.jcgame.shizhangmoniqi）中，以及您联系我们或访问政策网站时如何处理信息。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日。

1. 保存在设备上的信息

城市布局、财政、设施、工程、任职与剧情进度、游戏选择和奖励领取记录保存在本机。语言、音量和广告服务接受记录也保存在设备上。默认语言为英文，可在主菜单或游戏设置中选择简体中文。游戏没有玩家账号、开发者运营的云存档、玩家聊天、在线排行榜或真实货币内购。

游戏不会把完整存档发送到我们的游戏服务器。Android 系统备份或设备迁移可能依设备和平台设置复制或恢复应用数据。清除应用存储或卸载可能移除本地进度，但不一定删除系统备份。相关平台服务参阅 Google 隐私政策。

2. 广告服务与信息

当前 Android 版本包含 Unity LevelPlay、Unity Ads 和集成的 Ad Quality，用于激励广告、聚合及广告质量监测。这些服务可能处理广告、应用集、安装及其他假名化用户或设备标识，IP 地址及其推导的大致位置，设备、操作系统、应用和网络信息，广告请求、展示、点击、观看完成和奖励事件，广告表现及展示收益，广告素材元数据及技术诊断。

用途包括广告投放和衡量、奖励运行、质量监测、分析、故障排查、反欺诈、安全及合规。供应商可能依据其政策向服务提供商和广告合作伙伴披露信息，广告后台也可能向我们提供表现和质量报告。假名化信息不一定是匿名信息。

游戏不向广告 SDK 提供真实姓名、邮箱或自定义玩家账号 ID，也不申请精确位置、通讯录、相机或麦克风权限。由 IP 推断大致位置无需 GPS 权限。即使游戏没有账号，集成服务仍可能使用自己的标识及技术启动组件。

参阅 Unity 游戏玩家与应用用户隐私政策、Unity 玩家隐私常见问题及 LevelPlay 数据披露。

3. 广告与选择

核心单人玩法无需观看广告。未启用广告服务时，选择广告奖励会弹出说明。选择“暂不启用”、关闭提示或打开本政策不会启用激励广告，您可继续游戏。选择“启用”会保存接受记录，并允许游戏初始化和预加载广告；启用后，后续启动也可能自动初始化及预加载。拒绝某次奖励广告不代表所有广告服务数据处理停止。

游戏当前向 LevelPlay 传递 GDPR 个性化广告同意为否以及“不出售”信号。接受基础服务说明不等于同意个性化广告，这些设置也不代表情境广告、衡量、质量监测或安全处理停止。供应商处理受适用法律及可用隐私控制约束。

您可使用 Android 中可用的广告标识及广告隐私设置，以及供应商在广告中提供的隐私选项。清除游戏应用存储可重置接受记录，但同时会删除本地进度和偏好。卸载停止通过已安装游戏进行的后续处理，却不会自动删除供应商已有记录。有关记录可联系我们或使用供应商隐私渠道。详见 数据删除指引。

4. 客服、保留与删除

您主动发送邮件时，我们会收到邮箱地址、邮件正文及选择发送的附件，用于回复客服或隐私请求，并按处理需要及适用法律、安全或争议解决要求合理保留。请勿发送密码、银行卡信息或不必要的身份证明。

本地存档保留至设备上被替换或移除，另受系统备份行为影响。广告服务商按其自身规则保留信息。我们可协助识别相关服务商和请求途径；清除本地存档不会删除广告服务商已持有的信息。

5. 安全、跨境处理与权利

我们按所处理信息采取合理保护措施，请保护您的设备及备份。任何存储或传输方式都不能保证绝对安全。Unity、平台、托管和邮箱供应商可能依其政策及适用的跨境保护在您所在国家之外处理信息。

依适用法律，您可能享有访问、更正、删除、获取副本、限制或反对处理、撤回同意、退出出售或共享及向数据保护机构投诉的权利。我们处理的信息可联系我们；供应商独立控制的信息可使用其渠道。为定位及核验请求，可能需要有限信息。行使适用隐私权不会受到惩罚。

6. 年龄范围

游戏面向 13 岁及以上玩家，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。商店内容评级与隐私同意不同。如您认为儿童在未获所需授权时向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们调查并采取适当措施。

7. 其他服务与本网站

当前游戏未接入 Firebase Analytics 或 Crashlytics。后续增加分析、账号、购买或其他服务时，会更新政策及相关披露。剧情美术随应用提供，游玩时不调用在线 AI 服务生成。

本政策使用 GitHub Pages 托管，不添加广告、分析脚本、表单、跟踪像素或远程字体。GitHub 可能为托管及安全处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息，详见 GitHub 一般隐私声明。

8. 联系与更新

请通过 qijiangyuan@gmail.com 联系 Riverstar Games，主题注明 Mayor Journey Privacy。处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页及日期，并按要求提供进一步提示或选择。
