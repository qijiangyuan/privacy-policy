Privacy Policy for Sea Shelter: Ocean Survival

This policy applies to Sea Shelter: Ocean Survival, Android package com.jcgame.seashelter.global, provided by jcgame (we/us). Contact: qijiangyuan@gmail.com.

No game account is required. Camp progress and preferences are stored on your device. Advertising and analytics services, when active in your version and permitted by applicable privacy choices, process data separately from your local save.

1. Information processed

Local game data

The Game stores camp progress, inventory, settings, production and exploration state, offline timing, and reward limits locally. Privacy choices may also be stored locally by the consent service. We do not operate a game-account or cloud-save backend for this version. If you export a save, any copy you choose to share is under your control.

Analytics

We integrate Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve the Game. When collection is enabled, Google may process an app-instance identifier, device and operating-system information, app version, language, approximate region, and events such as first launch, app opens and session activity. Advertising identifiers may be processed when available and permitted by the corresponding consent and device settings. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity. We do not set a player account ID in Analytics or send your full local save to it.

Advertising and consent

We integrate Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) for advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for regional privacy messages. When advertising services are active, data can include IP address and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, device/app/network information, ad requests and interactions, and diagnostics. These are used for ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, security and service reliability. UMP processes consent choices and the technical information needed to determine and present the applicable message.

Service availability depends on the released version and configuration. Builds without active advertising or analytics do not generate the corresponding ad or analytics events. This policy does not imply that every SDK feature or every advertising network is active.

Support and permissions

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to attach, to respond and handle your request. The Game does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location for gameplay. This version has no active in-app payment or subscription service.

2. Regional choices and purposes

At startup, analytics collection remains off while regional privacy status is unresolved. Where prior consent is required, the relevant collection follows the choice returned by the consent process. In regions where prior consent is not required, analytics is enabled by default once that status is resolved, unless a denial applies. A failed or unresolved privacy check does not grant permission.

Analytics consent, advertising storage, use of data for advertising and ad personalization are handled separately. Permission to request an ad does not itself grant Analytics consent or permission for personalized ads. Where required by the regional consent service, open Settings → Privacy Options to review or change available choices. This entry appears only when the service requires it. Device Android privacy/ads settings can also restrict or delete your advertising ID; this alone does not disable all analytics or delete previously collected information.

Where applicable, processing relies on consent for activities requiring consent, providing requested functionality, legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention subject to your rights, or legal obligations. You may withdraw consent through available controls; withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact us to exercise rights or if you cannot find an applicable control.

3. Providers and sharing

Data may go directly from your device to Google or Unity/ironSource. We use their services to provide analytics, manage privacy choices and support advertising. Advertising disclosures may constitute sharing or targeted advertising under some laws. We do not sell your personal information for money. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or security, or in a lawful business transfer with appropriate safeguards.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

These public policy pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process normal web-request information, such as IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Retention, security and international processing

Local progress remains until you clear app storage or uninstall, subject to any copies or backups you control. Support records are kept only as needed to respond, document resolution and meet legal obligations. Provider-held analytics, advertising and consent records follow the applicable service retention settings and policies, including security and legal retention; aggregated data that no longer identifies you may remain. We do not promise a single retention period for all providers.

Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, with applicable transfer safeguards. We use reasonable protections and the providers' secure transmission mechanisms, but no system is completely secure.

5. Access, deletion and other rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of qualifying sale/sharing or targeted advertising. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact qijiangyuan@gmail.com; we may request proportionate information to verify and locate the relevant records. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

See Data Deletion Instructions. Clearing app data does not automatically delete historical provider-held data. Since no game account links your email to Analytics identifiers, we may need additional information to locate an SDK record and cannot guarantee identification from an email address alone.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in circumstances where parental permission is legally required without that permission. If you believe a child has provided personal data inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion or other appropriate action. Available features and privacy handling are subject to applicable age and regional requirements.

7. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when features, services or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest update; additional notice or consent will be provided where required. Developer: jcgame. Email: qijiangyuan@gmail.com.
