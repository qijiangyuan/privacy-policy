Delete data associated with Sea Shelter: Ocean Survival

Developer: jcgame. Android package: com.jcgame.seashelter.global.

There is no jcgame account or server-side cloud save to delete. You can delete local progress and request deletion of personal information associated with support, analytics or advertising.

1. Delete local progress

Open Android Settings → Apps → Sea Shelter: Ocean Survival → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage / Clear data. Uninstalling also removes the app's local data. This removes progress, settings, local reward records and locally stored privacy choices. Delete any separately exported saves or backups yourself. This action cannot restore your progress later.

2. Request deletion

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with subject Data Deletion Request – Sea Shelter: Ocean Survival. Include the game name, Android platform and the type of data you want deleted. For a support conversation, send from the original email address if possible. Do not send passwords, identity documents or payment details.

If records cannot be located from your request, we may ask for proportionate verification or a relevant device/app identifier and explain how to find it if available. Do not reset identifiers or clear app data before contacting us if you need help locating historical SDK data. There is no game account that automatically links your email to an Analytics app-instance ID.

3. What is deleted or retained

After verification, we delete or anonymize personal data under our control that is no longer required, and assist with requests to providers where applicable. Some information may remain as necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention or legal claims; non-identifying aggregated information may remain. Provider-held data follows each provider's policies and deletion processes. We respond within the applicable legal deadline and explain any applicable exceptions.

4. Future collection and provider data

Clearing local storage or uninstalling does not automatically erase prior Firebase or advertising records. Use Settings → Privacy Options where the regional service provides it to change available consent choices. Uninstalling stops future collection by this app on that device while uninstalled; reinstalling may create new identifiers. Android advertising-ID controls do not by themselves disable all Analytics collection.

For provider privacy controls, see Firebase Privacy and Security, Google Privacy Policy and Unity Player Privacy Policy. Contact us for help with records associated with this Game.

2. 发送删除请求

请向 qijiangyuan@gmail.com 发送主题为 Data Deletion Request – Sea Shelter: Ocean Survival 的邮件，说明游戏名、Android 平台及希望删除的数据类型。删除客服记录时，尽量使用原联系邮箱发送。请勿发送密码、身份证件或支付资料。

如无法定位记录，我们可能要求适当的核实信息或相关设备／应用标识符，并在可用时说明查找方式。如果需要协助定位历史 SDK 数据，请在重置标识符或清除数据前先联系我们。游戏没有把邮箱自动关联到 Analytics 应用实例 ID 的账号系统。

3. 删除及保留范围

核实后，我们会删除或匿名化由我们控制且不再需要的个人信息，并在适用时协助向服务商提出请求。为履行法律义务、安全、防欺诈或法律主张所需的信息可能继续保留，不再识别个人的汇总信息也可能保留。服务商持有的数据遵循其政策与删除流程。我们在适用法定期限内回复，并说明适用例外。

4. 后续采集与服务商数据

清除数据或卸载不会自动删除之前的 Firebase 或广告记录。地区服务提供入口时，可以在设置 → 隐私选项修改可用同意选择。卸载期间，本应用在该设备上的后续采集会停止；重新安装可能生成新的标识符。Android 广告 ID 设置本身不会关闭全部 Analytics 采集。

服务商信息见 Firebase 隐私与安全、Google 隐私政策及 Unity 玩家隐私政策。需要协助处理本游戏相关记录时，请联系我们。
