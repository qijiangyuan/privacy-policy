Delete your Storage Auction: CargoLot data

This page explains how users of Storage Auction: CargoLot (package name com.jcgame.jizhuangxiang.global) can delete locally stored game data or request deletion of other data associated with the Game. The Game is provided by jcgame.

The Game does not create a jcgame account and does not upload your saved game progress to a jcgame cloud server. You can still request deletion of information that may be processed in connection with advertising or support.
1

Delete local game data

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Select Apps, then Storage Auction: CargoLot.
  3. Select Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage or Clear data.

You can also delete local game data by uninstalling the Game.

2

Send a deletion request

Email us with the subject Data Deletion Request – Storage Auction: CargoLot. State that you use Android and describe the data you want deleted.

Email a deletion request

qijiangyuan@gmail.com

Information to include

  • The Game name: Storage Auction: CargoLot;
  • Your platform: Android;
  • The type of request, such as deleting advertising-related or support-related information;
  • A device or advertising identifier only if we later ask for it to locate relevant records.

Do not email us your password, payment-card number, bank information, or identity documents. We may need to verify that a request relates to you before acting on it. We normally respond within 30 days, or within the period required by applicable law.

Data deleted and data retained

Data deleted

  • Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes locally stored progress, settings, privacy choices, and local rewarded-ad state from that device.
  • Following a verified request, we will delete or anonymize personal information under our control that is not required to be retained.
  • Where applicable, we will help direct the request to a relevant provider or explain how to use that provider’s deletion controls.

Data that may be retained

  • The Game has no jcgame account or server-side cloud save to delete.
  • Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners may retain data under their own policies for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
  • We may retain deletion-request correspondence for up to 90 days after closing the request to document completion, unless law or a legal claim requires longer retention.
  • Aggregated or anonymized information that can no longer identify you may be retained.

Third-party advertising data

You can reset or delete the advertising identifier and manage ad privacy settings through Android. Unity also provides privacy controls and request information under its own policy. Because third-party providers control their own records, they may need to handle a request directly.

删除《Storage Auction: CargoLot》相关数据

生效及最近更新日期：2026 年 7 月 28 日

本页面说明 Storage Auction: CargoLot（软件包名称： com.jcgame.jizhuangxiang.global）用户如何删除本地游戏数据， 或请求删除与本游戏有关的其他数据。本游戏由 jcgame 提供。

本游戏不会创建 jcgame 账号，也不会将您的游戏存档上传至 jcgame 云服务器。 您仍可以请求删除因广告或客户支持而处理的信息。
1

删除本地游戏数据

  1. 打开 Android 设置；
  2. 选择应用，然后选择 Storage Auction: CargoLot；
  3. 选择存储和缓存；
  4. 点击清除存储空间或清除数据。

卸载本游戏也会删除本地游戏数据。

2

发送删除请求

请以 Data Deletion Request – Storage Auction: CargoLot 为主题向我们发送电子邮件，说明您使用 Android，并描述希望删除的数据。

发送删除请求邮件

qijiangyuan@gmail.com

邮件中需要提供的信息

  • 游戏名称：Storage Auction: CargoLot；
  • 使用平台：Android；
  • 请求类型，例如删除广告相关或客户支持相关信息；
  • 仅在我们随后要求用于查找相关记录时，提供设备或广告标识符。

请勿通过邮件发送密码、支付卡号、银行信息或身份证件。处理请求前，我们可能需要 核实该请求确实与您有关。我们通常会在 30 天内或适用法律规定的期限内回复。

删除和保留的数据

将删除的数据

  • 清除应用数据或卸载本游戏，会从该设备删除本地游戏进度、设置、隐私选择及本地激励广告状态；
  • 收到并核实请求后，我们会删除或匿名化由我们控制且无须继续保留的个人信息；
  • 在适用情况下，我们会协助将请求转交给相关服务商，或说明如何使用该服务商的删除功能。

可能保留的数据

  • 本游戏没有需要删除的 jcgame 账号或服务器端云存档；
  • Unity LevelPlay 及其广告合作方可能按照各自政策保留广告、衡量、防止欺诈、安全、争议解决或合规所需的数据；
  • 请求关闭后，我们可能将删除请求往来记录保留最多 90 天，以记录请求已完成；法律或法律主张要求更长时间的除外；
  • 无法再识别您身份的汇总或匿名化信息可能会继续保留。

第三方广告数据

您可以通过 Android 设置重置或删除广告标识符并管理广告隐私设置。Unity 也会 按照其隐私政策提供隐私控制和请求说明。由于第三方服务商自行控制其记录，相关 请求可能需要由该服务商直接处理。
