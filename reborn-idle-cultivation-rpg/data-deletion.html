Data Deletion (English)

This page applies to Reborn: Idle Cultivation RPG / 《开局废灵根，我无敌了》 by jcgame. The current Game has no player-account registration or jcgame cloud save.

Local saves, exported files, and online service records require separate deletion steps. Reincarnation does not delete all game data.

1. Clear data on your device

  1. Close the Game.
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Reborn: Idle Cultivation RPG → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data. Labels vary; the Game may appear as 开局废灵根，我无敌了.
  3. Confirm deletion. You may also uninstall the Game to remove app-local data.

This removes app-local progress, gameplay records, settings, the locally stored age group, and advertising choices. Clearing only the cache does not remove the save. Progress may be permanently lost. We cannot remotely clear or recover a save that exists only on your device. For a browser version, clear the relevant website's storage in browser settings.

2. Delete exports and other copies

Use your file manager to remove exported JSON saves and backups, including files named feilinggen-save-… or feilinggen-backup-before-import-…. Check Downloads, any cloud drive, other devices, and copies you shared. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not remove these files. The Game disables standard Android app backup; separately created migration or backup copies must still be managed at their saved locations.

3. Request deletion from jcgame

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject Reborn Idle Cultivation RPG Data Deletion. Describe the records you want deleted, when you used the relevant service, and the address you used to contact support, if applicable.

We may ask for limited details to identify records and verify the request. Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, or a complete game save. We will handle requests for information we hold, including support correspondence, and work with providers for data processed on our behalf. If records cannot be located, we will explain what information is needed.

4. Advertising and consent records

Google AdMob / UMP, Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Ad Quality, and Unity Ads may hold advertising or consent records. Clearing storage, uninstalling, withdrawing consent, or resetting an advertising ID does not automatically delete earlier records held by these services. For information they control independently, use the request routes in the Unity privacy policy and Google privacy policy. You can also contact us for help finding the appropriate route.

5. Timing and necessary retention

We respond within the period required by applicable law. We delete information covered by valid requests unless limited retention is necessary for a legal obligation, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. We will explain an applicable exception and limit retention to the necessary purpose and period. See the Privacy Policy for further information.

数据删除（简体中文）

本页适用于 jcgame 的 Reborn: Idle Cultivation RPG／《开局废灵根，我无敌了》，更新日期为 2026 年 9 月 12 日。当前游戏没有玩家账号注册或 jcgame 云存档。

本地存档、导出文件和在线服务记录需要分别删除。轮回不会删除全部游戏数据。

1. 清除设备上的数据

  1. 关闭游戏。
  2. 打开 Android 设置 → 应用 → Reborn: Idle Cultivation RPG／开局废灵根，我无敌了 → 存储与缓存 → 清除存储空间／清除数据。不同设备的菜单名称可能不同。
  3. 确认删除。您也可以卸载游戏以移除应用本地数据。

这会移除应用本地的进度、游戏记录、设置、本地年龄范围和广告选择。仅“清除缓存”不会删除存档。进度可能永久丢失；我们无法远程删除或恢复仅存在于您设备上的存档。对于浏览器版本，请在浏览器设置中清除对应网站的存储。

2. 删除导出文件及其他副本

使用文件管理器删除导出的 JSON 存档和备份，包括以 feilinggen-save-… 或 feilinggen-backup-before-import-… 命名的文件。请检查“下载”、云盘、其他设备及已分享的副本。清除应用数据或卸载不会移除这些文件。游戏关闭了标准 Android 应用备份；另行制作的迁移或备份副本仍需在保存位置管理。

3. 向 jcgame 申请删除

发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，主题注明 Reborn Idle Cultivation RPG Data Deletion／废灵根数据删除。请说明希望删除哪些记录、使用相关服务的大致时间，以及曾用于联系客服的邮箱（如适用）。

为定位记录和核实请求，我们可能要求有限的补充信息。请勿发送密码、支付资料、身份证件或完整存档。我们会处理自己持有的信息（包括客服往来），并就代表我们处理的数据与服务商配合。如果无法找到记录，我们会说明所需信息。

4. 广告及隐私同意记录

Google AdMob／UMP、Unity LevelPlay／ironSource、Ad Quality 和 Unity Ads 可能持有广告或隐私同意记录。清除存储、卸载、撤回同意或重置广告 ID，不会自动删除这些服务此前保存的记录。对于其独立控制的信息，请使用 Unity 隐私政策及 Google 隐私政策中的申请渠道，也可以联系我们协助确定合适的渠道。

5. 处理期限及必要保留

我们会在适用法律要求的期限内回复。有效请求所涉及的信息会被删除；履行法律义务、安全、防止欺诈或争议解决确需有限保留的除外。我们会解释适用的例外，并将保留限制在必要用途及期间。更多信息请参阅隐私政策。
