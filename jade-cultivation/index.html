English

This Privacy Policy explains how jcgame (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the international version of Jade Cultivation, also known as 青岚问道 (the “Game”), and when you contact us or visit this policy website.

The Android Game uses Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads with mediated networks including Google AdMob and Unity Ads, Google UMP for privacy choices, Google Analytics for Firebase subject to the choices described below, and Google Play Billing for Month Pass and Year Pass subscriptions. Earlier versions use AdMob directly; mediated AdMob and Unity Ads are available from version 1.08, Firebase from version 1.05, and subscriptions from version 1.09. Version 1.10 removes the optional Ad Quality SDK previously included in older LevelPlay releases, including 1.09. The practices that apply depend on the version and features you use.

1. Information we handle

Local game data. The Game stores progress, character development, equipment, gameplay records, settings, and privacy preferences on your device. Saving this information locally does not by itself upload your complete save to us. Device backup or restore features may also retain a copy under your device or platform settings.

Advertising and technical information. Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads, Google AdMob, and Unity Ads may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it; advertising, app, device, and, where applicable, account-related identifiers handled by the advertising provider; device and app information; interactions such as app launches, ad views, and taps; and diagnostic information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, service reliability, and fraud prevention. The information processed varies with your device, the services used, and applicable privacy settings.

Older-version diagnostics and installed-app information. Older LevelPlay releases, including version 1.09, included the optional Ad Quality SDK. In those versions, diagnostics may include information about applications installed on your device, such as application identifiers or whether particular apps are present, and related device or network environment information. This information may be transmitted to Unity / ironSource for ad-quality diagnostics, advertising measurement, service integrity, and fraud prevention. Version 1.10 removes this optional SDK. These older-version practices remain relevant if you continue using an older release; updating the Game does not automatically delete records already held by a provider.

Privacy preferences. Advertising privacy messages, including Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) where used, may process information needed to display the appropriate message and record or communicate your choices to supported services.

Analytics, when enabled. Google Analytics for Firebase may process app-instance or installation identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, device and app information, app launches, sessions, engagement, and rewarded-ad start, close, and result events. It may also process coarse location such as country or region, ad-source and impression-revenue information, and automatically collected purchase or subscription events containing product information, price, and currency. We use these reports to understand use of the Game, measure advertising performance, and improve usability and reliability. Collection follows the consent and local controls in Section 3. We do not intentionally add names, email addresses, passwords, complete saves, purchase tokens, or complete support messages to custom analytics events.

Subscriptions and purchase information. Google Play handles Month Pass and Year Pass payments. The Game and Google Play process product identifiers, purchase references or tokens, purchase state, purchase time, acknowledgement, and renewal information to confirm purchases, restore access, and provide subscription benefits. Payment details and Google-account authentication are handled by Google; the Game does not request your payment-card number or Google-account password. Purchase-related analytics, when enabled, are described above.

Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots, device details, or other information you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Why information is processed

  • To run the Game and remember your local progress and preferences.
  • To deliver and measure advertisements, verify rewarded-ad completion, and prevent invalid activity.
  • To understand gameplay and improve the Game when analytics is enabled.
  • To offer subscriptions, confirm payment status, and provide or restore Month Pass and Year Pass benefits.
  • To answer support and privacy requests, investigate issues, protect security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in support and security, legal obligations, or consent where required, including for certain advertising and analytics activities. Providers explain their own processing and legal bases in their policies. Reading or accepting this Policy does not by itself give consent to personalized advertising.

3. Advertising, analytics, and your choices

Rewarded advertisements are optional: you can decline an offer and continue playing without receiving that offer's reward. Ad requests, permitted preloading, and related processing may occur before you decide to watch an ad. Declining a rewarded ad does not by itself stop every advertising-related data request.

Where consent or an opt-out is required, the applicable privacy messages and choices will be provided. Use the privacy options available in your version of the Game to review those choices. You can also manage your advertising identifier and available ad-personalization controls in Android settings or use Google's ad controls. Contact us if you need help locating a relevant control.

In versions with Firebase Analytics, Google UMP manages the applicable consent choices. You can review available choices under Ad privacy options in the Game settings. Disabling ads in the Game also stops Firebase analytics collection. Advertising and analytics choices apply to separate purposes; permission to request an ad does not by itself grant analytics storage. After a successful privacy check confirms that consent is not required, newer versions enable basic analytics by default unless an explicit denial applies. Where analytics consent is required or unresolved, collection stays off until it is permitted. Analytics events may include ad-source and impression-revenue information.

Month Pass and Year Pass. An active subscription provides the available rewarded-ad benefits without watching a video, subject to the existing gameplay conditions and limits. Once an active Pass is confirmed, the Game stops preparing new rewarded ads for that subscription. Buying a Pass does not itself turn off Firebase Analytics or delete information already held by advertising or payment providers; use the separate privacy controls for analytics. Month Pass renews monthly and Year Pass renews yearly unless cancelled through Google Play. You can use Manage / cancel in the Game's Pass panel or Google Play's subscription settings. Clearing data or uninstalling the Game does not cancel a subscription.

Withdrawing consent affects future processing that relies on it. Non-personalized or limited ads may still use information needed for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention where permitted. Changing choices or uninstalling the Game does not automatically erase information already held by us or a provider.

The services covered by this Policy are:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads and Unity Ads: ad mediation, delivery, reporting, and fraud prevention. Older releases including 1.09 also include optional Ad Quality for the diagnostics described in Section 1; version 1.10 removes that component. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads (directly in earlier versions; mediated by LevelPlay from version 1.08): advertising delivery, reporting, and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.
  • Google UMP, where used: privacy messaging and consent management, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, when enabled: usage measurement and product improvement. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google Analytics data privacy and security.
  • Google Play and Google Play Billing: subscription checkout, purchase status, renewal, cancellation, and restoration of benefits. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's subscription help.
  • Email service providers: delivery and storage of communications you send to our support address.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of this privacy policy and the linked data-deletion page, as described in Section 9.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising-related processing may fall within broader legal definitions of “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising in some places. Where those rights apply, use the available privacy controls or contact us to request assistance.

Information may be processed by providers and their authorized service providers or advertising partners for the purposes described here and in their policies, subject to applicable choices. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, address fraud, or maintain security. We will update this Policy if new services materially change how information is handled.

5. Retention and deletion

Device-local data remains until you clear the Game's storage or otherwise remove it. Android's Clear storage / Clear data option removes app-local progress and settings, including local save backups. Uninstalling also removes app-local data, but system backups may remain or be restored according to your device settings. An in-game progress reset may not clear every preference or backup.

Support records are retained only for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal, security, or dispute-resolution requirements. Information no longer needed is deleted or anonymized.

Advertising and analytics data are retained according to the relevant service's policies and, where available, the retention settings for the service we use. When analytics is enabled, we limit retention to what is needed for the stated purposes. Data held by a provider is not automatically removed by clearing local game data.

Purchase and subscription records held by Google are subject to Google's policies and applicable payment, accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal requirements. Clearing the Game's local data does not delete Google Play transaction records, cancel renewal, or remove a paid subscription. Subscription management and privacy deletion requests are separate actions.

See our Data Deletion Instructions for local deletion steps and how to request deletion of information processed by us or on our behalf. We will assess and handle valid requests, including requests that require use of provider deletion tools. Limited retention may be necessary for legal obligations, security, or disputes; we will explain any applicable exception.

6. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including limiting access to support records and service accounts. Google Mobile Ads protects transmitted user data using TLS. Please protect access to your device and any backups. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google, GitHub, and other relevant providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their policies describe their processing locations, applicable transfer safeguards, and available rights. Where a transfer requires additional safeguards under applicable law, those safeguards apply.

7. Children and families

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13 or the applicable local age threshold. Parents and guardians should review the store's audience information and available device controls. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without the required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion or other protective steps. A store content rating is not consent to personal-data processing.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. You may also have rights to opt out of certain advertising-related processing.

Contact us to exercise rights concerning information we hold or process through our service providers. We may request limited information necessary to verify and locate the relevant data, and will respond within applicable legal time limits. We cannot remotely erase data that exists only on your device. For data controlled independently by Google or another provider, you may use that provider's privacy request channels; we can help identify the appropriate route. You will not be penalized for exercising applicable privacy rights.

9. This policy website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts to these policy pages. The Game's advertising and Firebase services, where enabled, are separate from this website. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Updates and contact

We will update this page and its date when our practices change, and provide additional notice or obtain consent when required. For privacy questions or requests, contact jcgame at qijiangyuan@gmail.com. Please use the subject Jade Cultivation Privacy.
