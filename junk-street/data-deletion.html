Junk Street: Shop Tycoon — Data deletion

The game has no player account or developer cloud-save service. Clear the app storage or uninstall to delete local progress, collection, language and advertising preferences. This cannot be undone from a developer server.

To disable future game-initiated advertising requests, turn off advertising consent in Settings and close the game. This does not erase data already sent to an advertising provider.

For privacy or deletion requests, contact Wuhan Jiangchen Game Technology Co., Ltd. at wuhanjiangchengame@163.com. Do not send passwords or identity documents. For provider-held advertising data, use the rights process in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Request scope, verification and retention exceptions depend on the provider and your jurisdiction.

删除本地数据及隐私申请

游戏没有玩家账号或开发者云存档。清除应用数据或卸载会删除本地经营进度、图鉴及偏好；开发者服务器无法恢复。设置中关闭广告选择并退出应用，可停止游戏继续请求广告，但不会删除广告供应商已持有的数据。隐私申请请联系武汉江辰游戏科技有限公司：wuhanjiangchengame@163.com。请勿发送密码或身份证件；第三方数据也可通过上方 Unity 政策的权利入口申请处理。
