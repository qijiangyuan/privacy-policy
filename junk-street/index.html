Junk Street: Shop Tycoon — Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 October 2026

Operator: Wuhan Jiangchen Game Technology Co., Ltd.

Contact: wuhanjiangchengame@163.com

1. Local game and your choices

You can play without an account, identity verification, or advertisements. The game stores progress, collection, scores, sound and other preferences on your device. It does not provide account login, cloud saves, purchases, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics. Your advertising choice is stored separately from gameplay saves. Opening this policy or an SDK policy does not enable ads.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

This build includes Unity LevelPlay mediation, ironSource Ads, the Unity Ads SDK and integrated Ad Quality for advertising and ad-quality monitoring. Only after you choose “Enable ads and continue” does the game initialize LevelPlay and preload rewarded advertisements; Ad Quality initializes with LevelPlay. Network availability and backend configuration determine the advertising sources used. This can cause network requests before you press a watch-ad button. Advertisements use internet access and may be unavailable offline. You may instead continue without ads and use the ordinary game actions.

The SDKs may process device and app information (such as operating system, device model, app/version, App Set ID and installation identifiers, advertising ID where available), IP address and approximate location derived from it, network information, ad impressions and interactions, ad-content metadata, and technical/performance information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, reward validation, ad-quality monitoring, fraud prevention and service operation. Advertising providers may process data on servers outside your country, retain it according to their policies, and share it with service providers and advertising partners as described there. The developer does not upload your local gameplay save to a game server through this integration.

This build requests non-personalized advertising settings: GDPR consent false and CCPA do-not-sell true, with child-directed treatment false for this non-child-directed game. These flags do not mean that an advertising SDK processes no data. We do not request a name, account password, telephone number, identity document, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location for these features.

3. Rewards

The two optional entries reveal the current item's appraisal clues or assist workbench processing. A genuine SDK reward callback and valid current game conditions are required. Closing an unrewarded ad, load failure, or unavailable service does not grant a reward. Normal game costs and game-day limits still apply; workbench success is not guaranteed.

4. Disable ads and delete local data

In Settings, disable advertising consent to stop new game-initiated ad requests and cancel pending game reward requests. You can keep playing locally. Withdrawal does not erase previously transmitted data or guarantee that an already initialized third-party SDK immediately stops every internal operation. Close the application after withdrawal if you want to end the running SDK process; subsequent starts keep ads disabled unless you explicitly enable them again. If saving your choice fails, the game tells you; retry saving the choice before restarting.

Device system settings let you reset or delete your advertising identifier where supported. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app storage deletes local saves and preferences on the device; this cannot delete data already held by an advertising provider. Device backup behavior is controlled by Android and the installed build; this version disables app backup. Contact the operator for privacy questions and use the provider's rights tools for provider-held data. There is no game account to delete.

5. Providers, retention, rights and changes

Local gameplay data stays until you delete it, replace a save, clear storage, or uninstall (subject to device behavior). For access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal, and other privacy requests, email the operator; rights depend on your jurisdiction. Data already processed by Unity/LevelPlay is governed by their current notices, retention practices and rights processes.
