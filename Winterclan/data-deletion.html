Data Deletion Request

Winterclan: Hex Tactics · 数据删除请求

How to request deletion

These instructions apply to the Android game Winterclan: Hex Tactics (package com.jcgame.winterclan.global).

  1. Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject Winterclan Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your in-game Player ID, selected server, and—if available—your Google Play Games player name or player ID. Tell us whether you want the entire account deleted or only specific information.
  3. We will acknowledge the request, verify account ownership where reasonably necessary, and complete the request within the period required by applicable law.
Quick action: Open a pre-filled email

Do not email your password, Google authorization code, purchase token, full payment-card details, or government ID. If additional verification is needed, we will explain a safer method.

Information deleted

After verification, deletion of the entire account removes or irreversibly de-identifies the jcgame account binding, player profile, gameplay progress, heroes, inventory, virtual items and currency, mail, event records, and other server-side gameplay data associated with the account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

You can remove local data separately through Android Settings → Apps → Winterclan: Hex Tactics → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the Game.

Information that may be retained

We may retain limited purchase, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal records when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service. Retained data will be limited, access-restricted, and used only for those purposes. Aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you may also be retained.

Third-party information

Deleting your jcgame account does not automatically delete data independently controlled by Google Play Games, Google Play, or Unity. Manage your Google account at Google Account and see the applicable provider's privacy controls for those records.

如何申请删除数据

本说明适用于 Android 游戏 Winterclan: Hex Tactics（包名：com.jcgame.winterclan.global）。

  1. 发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，邮件主题填写 Winterclan Data Deletion Request。
  2. 请提供游戏内玩家 ID、所选服务器，以及（如有）Google Play Games 玩家名称或玩家 ID，并说明希望删除整个账户还是特定信息。
  3. 我们会确认收到请求，在合理必要时验证账户归属，并在适用法律要求的期限内完成处理。
快捷操作：打开预填邮件

请勿通过邮件发送密码、Google 授权码、购买令牌、完整银行卡信息或政府身份证件。如需进一步验证，我们会说明更安全的方式。

将被删除的信息

验证通过后，删除整个账户会删除或不可逆地去标识化与该账户关联的 jcgame 账户绑定、玩家资料、游戏进度、英雄、背包、虚拟物品与货币、邮件、活动记录及其他服务器端游戏数据。此操作不可撤销，数据无法恢复。

您还可以通过 Android 设置 → 应用 → Winterclan: Hex Tactics → 存储 → 清除数据，或卸载游戏来移除本地数据。

可能保留的信息

法律要求或为保护服务而合理必要时，我们可能保留有限的购买、会计、安全、反欺诈、争议或法律记录。保留的数据将受到访问限制，并仅用于上述目的。无法合理识别您的汇总或去标识化信息也可能继续保留。

第三方信息

删除 jcgame 账户不会自动删除由 Google Play Games、Google Play 或 Unity 独立控制的数据。您可以前往 Google 账户管理 Google 数据，并使用相应服务商提供的隐私控制功能。
