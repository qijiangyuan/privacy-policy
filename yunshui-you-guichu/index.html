1. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores save-slot data locally, including game progress, any sect name you enter, a locally generated player identifier, resources, characters, unlocks, event history, virtual rewards, ad-reward counters, statistics, and timestamps. It also stores preferences such as language, music and sound settings, selected tracks, and consent-related choices.

These local game saves are used only on your device. We do not operate a player-account system, game backend, or cloud-save service for this release, and we do not receive your sect name, local player identifier, or save-file contents.

2. Information Processed by Google Services

Rewarded advertising and consent

The Android version of the Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide rewarded advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and honor consent and privacy choices. After applicable consent requirements are addressed, the advertising SDK may initialize and prepare an ad even if you do not ultimately choose to watch one.

Depending on your region, choices, device, and the advertisement served, Google and participating advertising providers may process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Advertising ID and other identifiers where available, for permitted purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention;
  • App Set ID and similar identifiers where available, for permitted non-advertising purposes such as analytics, security, and fraud prevention;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad requests, impressions, video views, clicks, and rewarded-ad completion;
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as app or SDK launch time, hangs, crashes, and energy usage; and
  • Device model, operating system, app version, language, and network information used for ad delivery, measurement, security, and diagnostics.

Firebase Analytics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics for standard app and session measurement and to understand whether rewarded-ad features are available and working. Analytics collection starts disabled and is enabled only when the consent state supplied through Google UMP permits collection.

When enabled, Firebase may assign an app-instance identifier and process standard app, session, device, and approximate-location information. The Game sends limited ad-related events, including the feature placement, daily request count, ad-ready state, result category, latency range, lifecycle stage, and a bounded error or response category. We do not intentionally send Firebase your sect name, local player identifier, save contents, email address, or other user-entered game text.

Information you send to us

If you email us for support, privacy questions, or a deletion request, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, government identification, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

For more information about Google’s processing, see Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.
