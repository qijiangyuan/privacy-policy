English

This Privacy Policy explains how jcgame (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Dice Tails: Roguelike RPG, also displayed as Dice Tails and 骰爪冒险团 (the “Game”), Android package com.jcgame.saizhuamaoxiantuan.global.

The Game does not require a player account or operate a cloud-save service. It includes support for optional rewarded advertising through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, with Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Advertising availability depends on the release and configuration. When advertising is disabled, the Game does not initialize these advertising and consent services.

1. Information handled by the Game

Local game data. Progress, heroes, equipment, achievements, local rankings, run history, language, sound settings, and other preferences are saved on your device. We do not receive these saves through a jcgame game server. This release has no account registration, online leaderboard, player chat, or cloud synchronization.

Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or other information you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate your request, and maintain necessary support records.

Advertising data, when enabled. Unity LevelPlay (including ironSource services), Unity Ads, and their advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them; advertising or other app/device identifiers; device model, operating system, language, app version, and network information; ad requests, impressions, clicks, reward events, and diagnostic data. Collection varies with your device, region, privacy choices, and the service used.

Consent information. When the privacy flow runs, Google UMP processes information needed to determine and present applicable privacy messages and record choices. Consent preferences are stored on the device and made available to supported advertising services.

The Game does not ask for access to your contacts, SMS, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. IP-based approximate location used by advertising services is different from precise device location.

2. Purposes and legal bases

We use local information to run the Game and remember your progress and preferences, and support information to answer requests. Advertising services use relevant information to deliver and measure ads, provide rewards, prevent fraud, and diagnose problems. Consent services help communicate and honor privacy choices.

Where applicable, processing relies on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in support and security, legal obligations, or consent when required for advertising or access to information on your device. Third-party providers explain their own legal bases in their policies. This Policy itself is not a request for advertising consent.

3. Advertising and your choices

Rewarded ads are optional: you can decline an offer and continue playing without that ad's reward. When advertising is enabled, privacy checks and permitted ad preloading can occur before you choose to watch an ad; declining a reward offer does not by itself prevent all advertising-related processing.

Where required, a privacy message is presented before advertising requests are permitted. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Privacy Options when that entry is shown. The Game's localized label may differ. You may also manage the advertising identifier and available ad privacy controls in Android settings. Changes do not automatically delete data already held by a provider.

Withdrawing consent affects future processing that depends on it. Limited or contextual ads may still involve data used for delivery, security, and measurement where permitted. Where applicable law provides sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising opt-outs, use the available privacy controls or contact us for assistance.

4. Providers, disclosures, and transfers

Relevant providers and their policies are:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads: advertising mediation, delivery, reporting, and fraud prevention. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google UMP: privacy messaging and consent management. See Google's Privacy Policy. UMP's presence does not mean this Game uses Google AdMob to serve ads.
  • Email services: delivery and storage of communications you send to our support address.

We do not sell personal information for money. Ad-related processing may fall within broader legal definitions of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising in some jurisdictions. Providers may disclose information to their service providers and advertising partners as described in their policies and subject to applicable choices. We may disclose support records when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

Providers may process information outside your country. Their policies describe applicable transfer mechanisms and safeguards. We do not operate our own international cloud-save service.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until removed from the device. To remove the Game's local progress, settings, and stored consent preferences, clear its storage through Android app settings or uninstall it. An in-game progress reset may leave settings, consent preferences, or local migration backups, so use Android's Clear storage option to remove all app-local data.

We retain support messages only as long as needed to handle the matter and meet applicable legal, security, or dispute-resolution requirements. Advertising and consent providers apply their own retention policies. Clearing app data does not delete information already held by them. See the Data Deletion Instructions for available steps and contact routes.

6. Security

We limit the information requested by the Game and access to support communications. Network services use the security protections provided by their platforms and SDKs. Please protect your device and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support requests. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Children and families

We do not knowingly ask children under 13, or the applicable local age threshold, to provide personal information directly to us. Parents and guardians should review the Game's store age information and available device controls. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion or other protective steps. A store content rating does not constitute consent to personal-data processing.

8. Privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. You can contact us to exercise rights relating to information we hold. We may request limited information to verify your request and will respond within applicable legal time limits.

We cannot remotely access or erase your device-local saves. For information held independently by Unity or Google, use the rights and contact options in their policies; we can help identify the relevant provider. You will not be penalized for exercising applicable privacy rights.

9. This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts to it. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Updates and contact

We will revise this page and its date when our practices change, and provide additional notice or request consent where required. For privacy questions or requests, contact jcgame at qijiangyuan@gmail.com, with “Dice Tails Privacy” in the subject.

简体中文 · 隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 9 月 5 日。本政策适用于 jcgame 提供的《骰爪冒险团》，英文名为 Dice Tails: Roguelike RPG，也显示为 Dice Tails，Android 包名为 com.jcgame.saizhuamaoxiantuan.global。

游戏无需注册账号，不提供云存档服务。游戏支持通过 Unity LevelPlay 和 Unity Ads 提供可选激励广告，并使用 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）管理隐私选择。广告是否可用取决于版本和配置；广告关闭时，游戏不会初始化这些广告及同意管理服务。

1. 游戏处理的信息

本地游戏数据：进度、英雄、装备、成就、本地排行榜、冒险记录、语言、音效和其他设置保存在您的设备上，不通过 jcgame 游戏服务器传给我们。当前版本没有账号注册、在线排行榜、玩家聊天或云同步。

主动联系：如果您发送邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容及您主动提供的截图或其他信息，用于回复、调查问题和保存必要的支持记录。

广告启用时：Unity LevelPlay（包括 ironSource 服务）、Unity Ads 及其广告合作伙伴可能处理 IP 地址及由此推断的大致位置、广告标识符或其他应用及设备标识符、设备型号、操作系统、语言、应用版本、网络信息、广告请求、展示、点击、奖励事件和诊断数据。具体处理取决于设备、地区、隐私选择及所用服务。

隐私选择：隐私流程运行时，Google UMP 处理判断和展示适用隐私消息、记录选择所需的信息。同意偏好保存在设备上，并供支持相应信号的广告服务使用。

游戏不请求访问通讯录、短信、麦克风、摄像头或精确 GPS 位置。广告服务根据 IP 推断的大致位置不同于设备精确定位。

2. 使用目的与处理依据

本地数据用于运行游戏和保存进度及设置；支持信息用于处理您的请求。广告服务使用相关数据投放和衡量广告、提供奖励、防止欺诈及诊断问题；同意管理服务用于传达并落实隐私选择。

在适用情况下，处理依据包括提供您请求的服务、支持与安全方面的合法利益、法律义务，以及广告或设备信息访问依法需要的同意。第三方在其政策中说明自己的处理依据。本政策本身不构成广告同意请求。

3. 广告与您的选择

激励广告由您选择观看，拒绝观看后仍可继续游戏，但不获得该次广告奖励。广告启用时，隐私检查和被允许的广告预加载可能在您选择观看前发生；拒绝奖励入口本身并不代表停止所有广告相关数据处理。

依法需要时，游戏会在允许请求广告前展示隐私消息。设置 → 隐私选项入口出现时，您可以重新查看可用选择，实际文字可能随语言而变化。您也可以通过 Android 设置管理广告标识符及可用广告隐私控制。更改选择不会自动删除服务商已持有的数据。

撤回同意会影响此后依赖该同意的处理。在法律允许时，有限广告或情境广告仍可能使用投放、安全及衡量所需的信息。若当地法律赋予出售、共享或定向广告退出权，请使用可用隐私控制或联系我们寻求协助。

4. 服务商、披露与跨境处理

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource、Unity Ads：广告聚合、投放、报告和反欺诈。参见 Unity 游戏玩家及应用用户隐私政策。
  • Google UMP：隐私消息与同意管理。参见 Google 隐私政策。使用 UMP 不代表游戏使用 Google AdMob 投放广告。
  • 邮件服务商：处理您向支持邮箱发送的通信。

我们不以金钱交换个人信息。某些地区对“出售”“共享”或“定向广告”的法律定义较广，广告相关处理可能属于这些定义。服务商可能按照其政策及适用隐私选择，向其服务供应商和广告合作伙伴披露信息。我们也可能依法或为维护权利及安全披露必要的支持记录。

服务商可能在您所在国家或地区之外处理信息，其政策说明适用的传输机制和保障措施。我们不运营自己的跨境云存档服务。
