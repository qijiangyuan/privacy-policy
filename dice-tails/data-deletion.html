English

Dice Tails does not require a jcgame player account or store game progress on a jcgame server. There is no game account to close. Your saved progress, rankings, and preferences are stored on your Android device.

1. Remove all local app data

  1. Close Dice Tails.
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Dice Tails → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data. Labels vary by device and language; the app may appear as 骰爪冒险团.
  3. Confirm the action. Alternatively, uninstall the Game to remove its app-local data.

This removes locally stored game progress, heroes, achievements, rankings, settings, migration backups, and saved app consent preferences. Progress is not recoverable from jcgame because we have no cloud save. In-game progress reset may retain some preferences or backups, so it is not a substitute for clearing all app storage.

2. Advertising information

If advertising services have been used, information may already be held by Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Unity Ads, or Google UMP. Clearing the Game's data, uninstalling, resetting an advertising identifier, or changing consent does not automatically erase provider-held data.

To review available choices, open Settings → Privacy Options in the Game when that option is displayed, or use Android's advertising privacy settings. For provider-held data, follow the request routes in Unity's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

3. Request deletion of information sent to jcgame

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject Dice Tails Data Deletion. Describe what you provided, roughly when, and which email address you used. Do not send passwords, government identification, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We may request limited verification and will respond within the time required by applicable law. We will delete personal information we hold that is covered by a valid request, except information that must be retained for legal obligations, security, or resolving disputes. Any exception will be explained and limited to the necessary purpose and period. We cannot remotely delete data stored only on your device or independently held by another provider.

简体中文 · 数据删除

适用游戏：《骰爪冒险团》／Dice Tails: Roguelike RPG。包名：com.jcgame.saizhuamaoxiantuan.global。更新日期：2026 年 9 月 5 日。

游戏无需注册 jcgame 玩家账号，不向 jcgame 服务器上传存档，因此没有需要注销的游戏账号。进度、排行榜与设置保存在您的 Android 设备上。

1. 删除全部应用本地数据

  1. 关闭游戏。
  2. 打开 Android 设置 → 应用 → Dice Tails／骰爪冒险团 → 存储与缓存 → 清除存储空间／清除数据。不同设备的名称可能不同。
  3. 确认清除。您也可以卸载游戏以移除应用本地数据。

这会移除本地进度、英雄、成就、排行榜、设置、迁移备份和应用保存的同意偏好。由于没有云存档，jcgame 无法恢复被删除的进度。游戏内重置进度可能保留部分偏好或备份，不能替代清除全部应用存储。

2. 广告相关信息

若曾使用广告服务，Unity LevelPlay／ironSource、Unity Ads 或 Google UMP 可能已经持有相关信息。清除应用数据、卸载、重置广告标识符或修改同意，不会自动删除服务商持有的数据。

游戏中设置 → 隐私选项入口出现时，可重新查看可用选择；也可使用 Android 广告隐私设置。涉及服务商持有的数据，请使用 Unity 隐私政策和 Google 隐私政策中的申请渠道。

3. 删除主动提供给 jcgame 的信息

请发送邮件至 qijiangyuan@gmail.com，主题注明“Dice Tails Data Deletion”或“骰爪冒险团数据删除”，说明所提供的信息、大致时间和使用的邮箱。请勿发送密码、身份证件或不必要的敏感信息。

我们可能要求有限的验证信息，并在适用法律要求的期限内回复。对于有效请求涉及且由我们持有的个人信息，我们将予以删除；法律义务、安全或争议解决所必需的保留除外。我们会说明例外，并将保留限制在必要目的和期限内。我们无法远程删除仅存于您的设备或由其他服务商独立持有的数据。
