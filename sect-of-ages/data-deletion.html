Delete data associated with Sect of Ages

Game: Cultivation Sim: Sect of Ages / 一宗千秋. Developer: jcgame. Android package: com.jcgame.cultivationsim.global.

There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save to delete. You can remove local progress and request deletion of personal information associated with support, analytics or advertising.

1. Remove local saves and backups

Use the Game's save-slot deletion control to delete that slot's local save and in-app backup. To remove all app data, open Android Settings → Apps → this Game → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage / Clear data. Uninstalling also removes local app data, subject to operating-system backup and restoration. Clearing storage removes all local slots, settings and stored privacy choices and cannot be undone unless you separately retained a backup.

Delete exported saves and photographs from their selected storage destination. Manage operating-system and cloud-provider backups separately through their controls; restoring a backup can restore old app data. Removing this Android app does not erase a separate browser version's local storage.

2. Request deletion of other information

Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request – Sect of Ages. Include the game name, Android platform and the data you want removed. For support correspondence, use your original contact email when possible. Do not send passwords, identity documents, payment details or an entire save unless specifically needed and requested.

We may ask for proportionate verification and a relevant app/device identifier if needed to locate records, explaining how to obtain it where possible. Your email is not automatically linked to an Analytics app-instance ID. If you need help identifying historical SDK records, contact us before clearing app data or resetting identifiers.

3. Deletion scope and exceptions

After verification, we delete or anonymize personal information under our control that is no longer needed, and assist with relevant Google or Unity/ironSource requests where possible. Providers apply their own deletion processes and retention policies. Information needed for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention or legal claims, and non-identifying aggregate information, may remain. We respond within the applicable legal deadline and explain any limits or exceptions.

4. Future collection and privacy choices

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not automatically erase past provider-held records. Where the regional consent service requires an entry, use Game Settings → Ad privacy options (仙境设置 → 广告隐私选项) to change available choices. Uninstalling stops further collection by this app on that device while uninstalled. Reinstalling can create new identifiers or restore backed-up data. Restricting or deleting the Android advertising ID does not by itself disable all Analytics collection.

See the Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, Google Privacy Policy and Unity Player Privacy Policy. Contact us for help with this Game's data.

请向 qijiangyuan@gmail.com 发送主题为 Data Deletion Request – Sect of Ages 的邮件，说明游戏名、Android 平台和希望删除的数据类型。处理客服往来时，尽量使用原联系邮箱发送。请勿发送密码、身份证件、支付资料；除非确有必要且经过请求，也不要发送完整存档。

4. 后续采集与隐私选择

清除应用数据或卸载不会自动删除服务商持有的历史记录。地区同意服务要求入口时，可在仙境设置 → 广告隐私选项修改可用选择。卸载期间，本应用在该设备上的后续采集会停止；重新安装可能生成新标识符或恢复备份数据。限制或删除 Android 广告 ID 本身不会关闭全部 Analytics 采集。

详情见隐私政策、Firebase 隐私与安全、Google 隐私政策和Unity 玩家隐私政策。需要本游戏数据处理协助时，请联系我们。
