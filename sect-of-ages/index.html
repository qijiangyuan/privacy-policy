Privacy Policy for Cultivation Sim: Sect of Ages

This policy applies to Cultivation Sim: Sect of Ages (also called Sect of Ages or 一宗千秋), Android package com.jcgame.cultivationsim.global, provided by jcgame (we/us). Contact: qijiangyuan@gmail.com.

The Game does not require an account. Your sect and save progress are stored on your device. Advertising and analytics services process technical and interaction data separately, subject to the features enabled in your version and applicable privacy choices.

1. Information processed

Game progress, exports and backups

The Game stores sect names, disciple and family information generated or entered in the Game, resources, construction and cultivation progress, preferences, offline timing, reward limits, save slots and local backups on your device. It does not automatically upload full saves, sect names or disciple names to a developer-operated game server. We do not operate an account or cloud-save backend for this version.

You can export saves or in-game photographs to a destination you select through Android. Files you export or share remain under your control. A cloud document provider you choose may process those files under its own policy. Android operating-system backup is allowed by the app configuration; availability, retention and restoration depend on your device and backup settings.

Google Analytics for Firebase

When analytics collection is enabled, Google may process app-instance and device identifiers, app and operating-system information, language, approximate region and usage events. Advertising identifiers may be processed where available and permitted by the corresponding choices and device settings. Such identifiers are pseudonymous; they do not make all data anonymous.

Our gameplay events describe session starts, panel types, building upgrades, construction, recruitment, breakthroughs, expedition completion, quest claims, sect stages and ascensions using counts, levels and fixed feature identifiers. Advertising events describe displays, earned and applied rewards, placement names, the network and format, and actual impression revenue reported by the ad SDK when available. We do not set a player account ID, send your full save or custom sect/disciple names to Analytics, or treat an in-game reward amount as advertising revenue.

Advertising, ad quality and consent

The Android Game integrates Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Unity Ad Quality, Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). When advertising services are active, they may process IP addresses and approximate location, advertising or other device/app identifiers, app/device/network information, ad requests, displayed ad information, views, clicks, rewards, and diagnostics. Purposes include delivering and measuring ads, personalization where permitted, monitoring ad content and quality, preventing fraud and maintaining service security and reliability.

UMP processes privacy choices and technical information needed to determine and display regional consent messages, and stores choices locally. No precise GPS permission is requested for this process. Ad and analytics availability depend on your app version, service configuration and applicable choices. Not every feature of these providers is used, and a refusal of personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all ad-related processing.

Support and permissions

If you contact us by email, we receive your address, message and any attachments you provide to handle your request. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information. The Game does not request contacts, microphone, camera or precise location access for gameplay. This version has no game login, active in-app purchase or subscription service, or Firebase Crashlytics integration.

2. Regional privacy choices

Analytics collection is paused while the startup regional check is unresolved. Where prior consent is required, collection follows the applicable analytics choice. When UMP successfully confirms that prior consent is not required, analytics is enabled by default unless an explicit denial applies. An unknown result does not grant new permission; a valid existing privacy choice may be used if a refresh fails.

Analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization are handled separately. Being allowed to request ads does not itself mean you agreed to analytics or personalized advertising. Where UMP requires a privacy entry, open the Game's Settings → Ad privacy options (Chinese: 仙境设置 → 广告隐私选项) to review or change available choices. The entry appears when the regional service requires it. Contact us if you need assistance or cannot find an applicable control.

Android privacy/advertising settings may let you restrict or delete your advertising ID. This does not by itself disable every analytics feature or erase historical records. Withdrawing consent affects future processing and does not change the lawfulness of prior processing. Depending on the purpose and applicable law, processing relies on consent, providing requested functionality, legitimate interests such as service security and fraud prevention subject to your rights, or legal obligations.

3. Providers and sharing

Technical and interaction data may be sent directly from your device to Google and Unity/ironSource and processed by their service providers and applicable advertising partners for the purposes above. Ad-related disclosures may qualify as sharing or targeted advertising under some laws. We may disclose information to meet a legal requirement, protect rights or security, or carry out a lawful business transfer with appropriate safeguards.

  • Google Privacy Policy — AdMob, Google services and support email.
  • Firebase Privacy and Security and Analytics data collection.
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy — LevelPlay/ironSource and Ad Quality.

This policy website contains no advertising, tracking scripts, analytics tools, forms or remote fonts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process normal request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Retention, security and international processing

Local progress remains until you delete its save slot, clear app storage or uninstall, subject to backup and restore behavior. Exported files and system/cloud backups must be removed separately. We retain support correspondence only as needed to respond, document resolution and meet legal obligations. Provider-held analytics, advertising and consent information follows the applicable retention settings and provider policies; necessary legal/security records and non-identifying aggregate data may remain.

Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under applicable transfer safeguards. We use reasonable protections and provider-supported secure transmission, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and any exported save files.

5. Your rights and deletion

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction; object to processing; withdraw consent; opt out of qualifying sale/sharing or targeted advertising; or complain to a local data protection authority. Email qijiangyuan@gmail.com. We may request proportionate verification information and respond within the applicable legal deadline.

See Data Deletion Instructions. There is no account linking your email to an Analytics app-instance identifier, so an email alone may not identify historical SDK records. Clearing local storage does not automatically delete provider-held information. We assist with relevant provider requests where possible and explain applicable limits or legal exceptions.

6. Children's information

The Game does not ask for a real name or date of birth. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances requiring your permission, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable. Please avoid entering real-world personal information in sect or disciple names or support attachments. Age eligibility and privacy requirements vary by location.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest update; additional notice or consent will be provided where required. Developer: jcgame. Privacy contact: qijiangyuan@gmail.com.

《一宗千秋》隐私政策

本政策适用于 Cultivation Sim: Sect of Ages（亦称 Sect of Ages／一宗千秋），Android 包名 com.jcgame.cultivationsim.global，由 jcgame 提供。生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日。联系邮箱：qijiangyuan@gmail.com。

游戏无需注册账号，宗门和存档进度保存在本机。广告与统计服务会根据相应版本启用的功能及适用隐私选择，独立处理技术和交互数据。

1. 处理的信息

游戏进度、导出与备份

游戏在本机保存宗门名、游戏生成或您在游戏内输入的弟子及家族资料、资源、建筑与修行进度、偏好、离线计时、奖励次数限制、存档槽及本地备份。游戏不会自动将完整存档、宗门名或弟子名上传至开发者运营的游戏服务器。当前版本没有游戏账号或云存档后端。

您可以通过 Android 选择保存位置，导出存档或游戏摄影图片。导出与分享的文件由您控制；如果选择云端文件提供商，对方会按照自己的政策处理文件。应用配置允许 Android 系统备份，其可用性、保留和恢复取决于设备与备份设置。

Google Analytics for Firebase

启用统计后，Google 可能处理应用实例及设备标识符、应用与操作系统信息、语言、大致地区和使用事件。在相应选择及设备设置允许且标识符可用时，也可能处理广告标识符。这些标识符属于假名化信息，并不表示所有数据完全匿名。

自定义玩法事件通过次数、等级和固定功能标识描述会话开始、面板类型、建筑升级、营造、招募、突破、历练结算、目标领奖、宗门升阶和飞升。广告事件描述展示、获得和实际发放奖励、广告点、网络、格式，以及广告 SDK 提供的真实展示收益（如有）。我们不设置玩家账号 ID，不向 Analytics 发送完整存档、自定义宗门名或弟子名，也不把游戏奖励数量当成广告收益。

广告、广告质量与同意管理

Android 游戏集成 Unity LevelPlay／ironSource、Unity Ad Quality、通过 Google Mobile Ads SDK 接入的 Google AdMob 及 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）。广告服务启用时，可能处理 IP 地址和大致位置、广告或其他设备／应用标识符、应用／设备／网络信息、广告请求、展示广告信息、观看、点击、奖励及诊断。用途包括广告投放与衡量、在获准时进行个性化、监测广告内容与质量、防欺诈、安全和服务可靠性。

UMP 处理隐私选择及确定、展示适用地区同意消息所需的技术信息，并在本机保存选择。该流程不申请精确 GPS 定位权限。广告与统计是否可用取决于应用版本、服务配置及适用选择；并非所有服务商功能都被启用，拒绝个性化广告也不一定停止所有广告相关数据处理。

客服与权限

通过邮箱联系我们时，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、消息和主动提供的附件，用于处理请求。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。游戏玩法不申请联系人、麦克风、摄像头或精确定位权限。当前版本没有游戏登录、已启用的应用内购买或订阅服务，也未集成 Firebase Crashlytics。

2. 地区隐私选择

启动地区检查尚未确定时暂停统计采集。需要事先同意的地区按适用的统计选择采集；UMP 本次成功确认无需事先同意时，默认开启统计，但不会覆盖明确拒绝。未知结果不授予新的许可；刷新失败时，可能沿用有效的既有隐私选择。

分析存储、广告存储、广告用户数据和广告个性化分别处理。允许请求广告不等于同意统计或个性化广告。UMP 要求提供隐私入口时，可在游戏的仙境设置 → 广告隐私选项查看或修改可用选择；该入口仅在地区服务要求时显示。如需要协助或找不到适用控件，请联系我们。
