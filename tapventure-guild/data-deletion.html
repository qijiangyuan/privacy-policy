Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceData Deletion
Tapventure Guild does not use player accounts or store game progress on jcgame servers. Your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your Android device.

Delete Local Game Data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Tapventure Guild → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Menu names can vary by device. You can also uninstall the Game. Either action permanently removes locally stored progress and settings from that device.

Advertising Data and Privacy Choices

Advertising and consent information may be processed by Google independently of jcgame. When the option is required and available, open the Game’s Settings → Privacy Choices to review your advertising consent choices. You can also manage advertising privacy settings in Android and review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

Because jcgame has no player account or cloud save for this Game, we normally cannot identify or delete device-local game data on your behalf. If you believe you supplied personal information directly to us, email qijiangyuan@gmail.com with the subject “Tapventure Guild Data Deletion.” Describe the information and how it was provided. Do not send passwords, government identification, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What Happens Next

We will acknowledge a verifiable request and respond within the period required by applicable law. We may need limited information to confirm the request. Information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be kept only as long as required.
